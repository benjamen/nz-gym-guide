How to Get a Free Gym Trial in NZ

Getting a free trial at a New Zealand gym is straightforward — most major chains actively want you to try the gym before you sign up. Here's how to do it:

  • Book online first. Jetts, Anytime Fitness, Snap Fitness and City Fitness all have a "free trial" or "guest pass" form on their websites. Fill it in and you'll usually get a pass within minutes.
  • Bring photo ID. Almost every gym requires ID to activate a trial pass — it stops people claiming repeat trials.
  • No credit card needed (mostly). Jetts is explicit about no card required. ClassPass is the exception — it asks for a card for its free trial month, so set a reminder to cancel before it ends.
  • One trial per person. You generally can't trial the same chain twice, but you can trial several different chains back-to-back to compare them for free.
  • Ask about joining-fee waivers. If you join right after a trial, many clubs will waive the joining fee — always ask.

Tips for Using Free Trial Passes

  • Go at your real workout time. Trial during the hours you'd actually train — a gym that's empty at 11am can be packed at 6pm.
  • Try the classes. A free trial usually includes group fitness — use it to test whether the class timetable suits you.
  • Test the commute. The best gym is the one you'll actually get to. Use the trial to see how the trip home from work feels.
  • Stack your trials. Trial two or three nearby gyms in the same week, then sign up to the one that fits — all for free.
  • Don't sign on the spot. Take the trial, sleep on it, then negotiate. Sales staff often have room to waive fees if you don't rush.

Free Gym Trials NZ — FAQ

Which NZ gyms offer a free trial?

Jetts, Anytime Fitness, Snap Fitness and City Fitness all offer 7-day free trials. Les Mills and Orangetheory offer a free intro class, and ClassPass offers a free trial month across 500+ NZ studios. Many F45 studios offer a free trial week.

Can I get a free gym trial with no credit card in NZ?

Yes. Jetts' 7-day trial requires no credit card, and free intro classes at Les Mills and Orangetheory need no card either. ClassPass is the main exception — it requires a card for its free trial month.

How much is a casual gym visit in NZ?

Casual visits at chain gyms typically cost $15–25. Council and community leisure centres are much cheaper at around $3–8 for casual gym or pool entry.

Can I do more than one free trial?

You usually can't trial the same chain twice, but you can trial several different gyms one after another — a great way to compare gyms in your area for free before committing.
