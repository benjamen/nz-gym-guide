Tauranga Gyms

Tauranga's fitness scene is thriving with several gyms to choose from. City Fitness offers the most affordable option, while Anytime Fitness provides a no-contract membership. The Exercise NZ subsidy scheme can help with 40-70% off for eligible members.

Updated May 2026 5 min read

Cheapest Gyms in Tauranga

City Fitness is the cheapest gym option in Tauranga, with a price of $18.99/week on a month-to-month basis. Anytime Fitness also offers a no-contract membership with 24/7 access to over 5,000 clubs worldwide.

The Exercise NZ subsidy scheme is a great way to get the biggest discount, with 40-70% off for eligible members. This scheme is available for new members or those who have been inactive for 12+ months.

No-Contract Options

Anytime Fitness and Snap Fitness offer no-contract membership options in Tauranga. These memberships provide flexibility and convenience, allowing you to cancel at any time.

City Fitness also offers a month-to-month membership, which is a great option for those who don't want to commit to a long-term contract.

  • Anytime Fitness: no-contract membership with 24/7 access
  • Snap Fitness: month-to-month membership
  • City Fitness: month-to-month membership

Exercise NZ Subsidy Scheme

The Exercise NZ subsidy scheme is a great way to save money on your gym membership. Eligible members can receive 40-70% off their membership, making it more affordable to stay active and healthy.

To be eligible for the subsidy scheme, you must be a new member or have been inactive for 12+ months. Contact your local gym to find out more and to see if you're eligible.
