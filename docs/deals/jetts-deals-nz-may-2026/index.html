Jetts NZ Deals

Jetts NZ is a budget-friendly gym option with no joining fee and flexible membership plans. Current deals include 15% off membership plus 4 weeks free on 12 or 18-month terms. With prices starting from $14.95–$19.95/week, Jetts NZ is an affordable choice for those seeking flexibility and 24/7 access.

Updated May 2026 5 min read

Current Promotions and How to Claim

Jetts NZ is currently offering 15% off membership plus 4 weeks free on 12 or 18-month terms. To claim this deal, simply sign up for a Jetts NZ membership on their website or in-club. No promo code is required, and the discount will be applied automatically.

In addition to this deal, Jetts NZ also offers a no-joining-fee policy, making it an attractive option for those who want to try out a gym without committing to a long-term contract.

  • 15% off membership
  • 4 weeks free on 12 or 18-month terms
  • No joining fee

Best Time to Join and Comparison to Exercise NZ Subsidy

The best time to join Jetts NZ for deals is typically during promotional periods, such as New Year's sales or special events. However, with the current 15% off and 4 weeks free deal, it's always a good time to sign up.

In comparison to the Exercise NZ subsidy scheme, which offers 40–70% off for eligible members, Jetts NZ's prices are still competitive, especially with the current promotion. However, those who are eligible for the Exercise NZ subsidy may find it a more affordable option in the long run.

Tips for Getting the Best Deal on a Jetts Membership

To get the best deal on a Jetts NZ membership, consider the following tips:

  • Sign up during promotional periods for discounts and free trials
  • Choose a longer contract term to get a lower weekly rate
  • Consider the Exercise NZ subsidy scheme if you're eligible
  • Negotiate with the club staff to see if they can offer any additional discounts
  • Use online coupons or promo codes if available
