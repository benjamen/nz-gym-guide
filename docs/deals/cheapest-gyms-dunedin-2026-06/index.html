Dunedin's Cheapest Gyms

Dunedin boasts a range of gym options catering to different budgets and preferences. The city's gym scene is particularly appealing to students, with the YMCA offering one of the best student gym deals in New Zealand. For those seeking affordability without long-term commitments, several no-contract options are available.

Updated June 2026 5 min read

Most Affordable Options in Dunedin

The YMCA stands out as the cheapest option, providing a cost-effective way to stay fit. Prices start from around $15 per week for a basic membership, although prices can vary depending on the time of year and membership type. Another affordable option is Snap Fitness, which occasionally offers promotions like 6 weeks free. Anytime Fitness is a good choice for those who prefer a no-contract arrangement, with 24/7 access to facilities.
  • YMCA: from $15/week
  • Snap Fitness: 6 weeks free
  • Anytime Fitness: no-contract, 24/7 access

No-Contract Alternatives

For individuals who prefer the flexibility of not being locked into a long-term contract, Dunedin has several options. Anytime Fitness is a prominent choice, offering access to over 5,000 clubs worldwide with no lock-in contract. Les Mills also provides a Flexi Membership with no lock-in contract, although it might be pricier than some other options. The Exercise NZ subsidy programme can significantly reduce membership costs for eligible members, making these options even more affordable.
  • Anytime Fitness: 24/7 access, no contract
  • Les Mills: Flexi Membership, no lock-in contract

Exercise NZ Subsidy

The Exercise NZ subsidy programme offers the most significant discount available for gym memberships in Dunedin, with savings of 40-70% off regular membership prices for eligible members. This subsidy is particularly beneficial for those who are new to gym memberships or have been inactive for over 12 months. It's worth checking eligibility for this programme before committing to any gym membership.
  • Savings: 40-70% off regular prices
  • Eligibility: new members or inactive for 12+ months
