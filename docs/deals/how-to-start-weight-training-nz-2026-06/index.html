Start Weight Training

Weight training is an excellent way to improve overall fitness and health. As a beginner, it's essential to start with the right equipment and techniques. NZ gyms offer a range of options, from free weights to machines, to help you achieve your fitness goals.

Updated June 2026 7 min read

Free Weights vs Machines

When it comes to weight training, you have two primary options: free weights and machines. Free weights, such as dumbbells and barbells, provide a more comprehensive workout and help improve balance and coordination. Machines, on the other hand, target specific muscle groups and can be easier to use for beginners. City Fitness and Les Mills gyms in NZ have well-equipped free weights areas, while Jetts and Anytime Fitness have a range of machines to choose from.
  • Free weights: improve balance and coordination
  • Machines: target specific muscle groups, easier to use for beginners

Compound Movements

Compound movements are exercises that work multiple muscle groups at once, such as squats, deadlifts, and bench press. These movements are essential for building overall strength and muscle mass. As a beginner, it's crucial to master these movements to progress in your weight training journey. Many NZ gyms, including City Fitness and Les Mills, offer group fitness classes that focus on compound movements.
  • Squats: work legs, glutes, and core
  • Deadlifts: work back, legs, and core
  • Bench press: work chest, shoulders, and triceps

Progressive Overload

Progressive overload is the gradual increase of weight or resistance to challenge your muscles and promote growth. This can be achieved by increasing the weight, reps, or sets over time. It's essential to track your progress and adjust your workout routine accordingly. Anytime Fitness and Jetts gyms in NZ have a range of equipment to help you progress in your weight training journey.
  • Increase weight: challenge muscles and promote growth
  • Increase reps: improve muscular endurance
  • Increase sets: improve overall strength and muscle mass

Best NZ Gyms for Weight Training

When it comes to choosing the best NZ gym for weight training, consider the equipment, group fitness classes, and personal training options. City Fitness and Les Mills gyms have well-equipped free weights areas and offer a range of group fitness classes. Jetts and Anytime Fitness have a range of machines and equipment to suit all fitness levels.
  • City Fitness: well-equipped free weights area, group fitness classes
  • Les Mills: well-equipped free weights area, group fitness classes
  • Jetts: range of machines and equipment, 24/7 access
  • Anytime Fitness: range of machines and equipment, 24/7 access

Conclusion and Next Steps

Starting weight training can seem daunting, but with the right equipment, techniques, and mindset, you can achieve your fitness goals. Remember to start with compound movements, progress gradually, and choose a gym that suits your needs. Take the first step towards a healthier and fitter you.
