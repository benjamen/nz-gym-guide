Tauranga Gym Deals

Tauranga's sunny lifestyle means gym culture is strong. City Fitness has several locations across the Bay of Plenty, and the area has a growing boutique fitness scene including F45. With numerous gyms to choose from, it can be challenging to find the best value for your money. Here's a rundown of the current deals and offers available in Tauranga.

Updated May 2026 5 min read

Current Gym Deals in Tauranga

Several gyms in Tauranga are offering attractive deals to new members. City Fitness is currently the cheapest option, with a month-to-month membership for $18.99/week. Les Mills is also offering a flexi membership with no lock-in contract. Anytime Fitness provides 24/7 access to over 5,000 clubs worldwide.

Snap Fitness is giving away 6 weeks free, while F45 offers a free trial week. Eligible members can also take advantage of Exercise NZ's subsidy scheme, which offers 40-70% off gym memberships.

  • Les Mills: Flexi membership (no lock-in contract)

  • Snap Fitness: 6 weeks free

Best Value Gyms in Tauranga

City Fitness is currently the best value gym in Tauranga, with a month-to-month membership for $18.99/week. Les Mills is the premium pick, offering a range of group classes and high-end facilities.

Money-Saving Tips for Joining a Gym in Tauranga

Here are some tips to help you save money when joining a gym in Tauranga:

  • Take advantage of the Exercise NZ subsidy scheme (40-70% off for eligible members)
  • Consider a month-to-month membership instead of a long-term contract
  • Look for gyms that offer free trials or discounts for new members
  • Negotiate with the gym to see if they can offer any discounts or promotions
  • Use online coupons or promo codes to get a discount on your membership
