Exercise NZ Subsidy Scheme

The Exercise NZ subsidy scheme offers 40-70% off gym memberships for eligible members, including new members or those who have been inactive for 12 months or more. This scheme can help make gym memberships more affordable and accessible to a wider range of people.

To be eligible for the subsidy, members must meet certain criteria, such as being a New Zealand citizen or resident, and not having been a gym member in the past 12 months.

Money-Saving Tips for Joining a Gym in Hamilton

When joining a gym in Hamilton, there are several ways to save money. Firstly, consider taking advantage of the Exercise NZ subsidy scheme if you are eligible. Secondly, look for gyms that offer month-to-month memberships or no-contract options, as these can provide more flexibility and avoid long-term commitments.

Thirdly, compare prices and features between different gyms to find the best value for your needs and budget. Fourthly, consider joining a gym during off-peak hours or during promotions and discounts. Finally, be sure to read the fine print and understand the terms and conditions of your membership contract before signing up.

  • Look for month-to-month memberships or no-contract options
