Les Mills vs PRJCT

Les Mills and PRJCT CrossFit NZ are two popular gym options in New Zealand, catering to different fitness needs and preferences. Les Mills is a premium gym with a wide range of facilities and group fitness classes, while PRJCT CrossFit NZ is a boutique gym specialising in CrossFit training. In this comparison, we'll help you decide which gym is best for you.

Updated May 2026 5 min read

Overview of Les Mills and PRJCT CrossFit NZ

Les Mills is a premium gym with multiple locations across New Zealand, offering a variety of facilities including group fitness classes, pool, sauna, and personal training. PRJCT CrossFit NZ, on the other hand, is a boutique gym with a focus on CrossFit training and a community-driven approach. Both gyms have their unique features and benefits, but they also have some key differences that may make one more suitable for your needs than the other.
  • Les Mills: group fitness classes, pool, sauna, personal training
  • PRJCT CrossFit NZ: CrossFit training, community-driven approach

Comparison of Key Features

When it comes to pricing, Les Mills offers a more affordable option with prices starting from $17.99 per week, while PRJCT CrossFit NZ is priced from $45.00 per week. Les Mills also has a joining fee of $49, which is often waived in promotions. In terms of contract length, Les Mills requires a minimum term, while PRJCT CrossFit NZ has no lock-in contract.

Verdict and Recommendation

Based on our comparison, Les Mills is the better option for those who value a wide range of facilities and group fitness classes, while PRJCT CrossFit NZ is ideal for CrossFit enthusiasts who prefer a community-driven approach. It's also worth considering the Exercise NZ subsidised gym scheme, which offers 40-70% off for eligible members. Ultimately, the choice between Les Mills and PRJCT CrossFit NZ depends on your individual fitness goals and preferences.
  • Les Mills: best for group fitness and premium facilities
  • PRJCT CrossFit NZ: best for CrossFit enthusiasts and community-driven approach
