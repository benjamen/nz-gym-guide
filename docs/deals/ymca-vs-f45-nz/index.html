YMCA vs F45

YMCA and F45 Training NZ are two popular gym options in New Zealand, catering to different fitness needs and budgets. YMCA offers a community-focused approach with affordable prices, while F45 provides a premium, boutique experience. In this comparison, we'll help you decide which gym is best for you.

Updated May 2026 5 min read

Overview of YMCA and F45 Training NZ

YMCA NZ is a community-focused gym with prices starting from $17.50 to $26.00 per week, featuring group fitness classes, a pool, personal training, and crèche facilities. F45 Training NZ, on the other hand, is a boutique gym with prices ranging from $25.00 to $42.50 per week, offering high-intensity interval training (HIIT) classes and a free trial week.

Both gyms have their strengths and weaknesses. YMCA is ideal for families and those seeking a community atmosphere, while F45 is suited for individuals who prefer group motivation and fast results.

Price and Value Comparison

When comparing prices, YMCA is the more affordable option, with prices starting at $17.50 per week. F45 Training NZ, however, offers a premium experience with prices ranging from $25.00 to $42.50 per week.

Consider the Exercise NZ subsidy scheme, which offers 40-70% off for eligible members. This can significantly reduce the cost of your gym membership.

  • YMCA: $17.50-$26.00/week
  • F45 Training NZ: $25.00-$42.50/week
  • Exercise NZ subsidy: 40-70% off for eligible members

Facilities, Classes, and Locations

YMCA NZ features a range of facilities, including group fitness classes, a pool, personal training, and crèche. F45 Training NZ, on the other hand, focuses on HIIT classes and offers a free trial week.

YMCA has limited locations, while F45 Training NZ has a wider reach across New Zealand.

  • YMCA: group fitness, pool, personal training, crèche
  • F45 Training NZ: HIIT classes, free trial week
  • YMCA: limited locations
  • F45 Training NZ: nationwide locations

Verdict and Recommendation

Based on our comparison, YMCA is the best choice for families and those seeking a community-focused gym experience on a budget. F45 Training NZ, however, is ideal for individuals who prefer a premium, boutique experience and are willing to pay for it.

For those prioritizing affordability, YMCA is the better option. For those seeking a high-end experience, F45 Training NZ is the way to go.
