Snap Fitness NZ Deals

Snap Fitness NZ offers affordable and convenient 24/7 gym access, with prices starting from $14.95–$17.95/week. Current promotions include up to 6 weeks free on 12-month memberships. AIA Vitality members are also eligible for exclusive deals.

Updated May 2026 5 min read

Current Promotions

As of May 2026, Snap Fitness NZ is offering up to 6 weeks free on 12-month memberships. This deal is available for new members, and AIA Vitality members can also take advantage of exclusive promotions.

In addition to these deals, Snap Fitness often runs joining fee waiver promotions, which can save you $49. Keep an eye on their website or social media for the latest offers.

  • Up to 6 weeks free on 12-month memberships
  • Joining fee waiver promotions available

Best Time to Join

The best time to join Snap Fitness NZ for deals is usually during promotional periods, such as New Year's sales or summer specials. Keep an eye on their website and social media for the latest offers.

Additionally, consider joining during the off-season (usually October to December) when gyms may offer discounts to attract new members.

  • New Year's sales
  • Summer specials
  • Off-season (October to December)

Joining Fee and Exercise NZ Subsidy

The joining fee for Snap Fitness NZ is $49, but it can often be waived during promotional periods. If you're eligible for the Exercise NZ subsidy scheme, you may be able to get 40-70% off your membership, which could be a more cost-effective option.

To be eligible for the Exercise NZ subsidy, you must be a new member or have been inactive for 12+ months.

  • Joining fee: $49 (can be waived)
  • Exercise NZ subsidy: 40-70% off for eligible members

Tips for Getting the Best Deal

To get the best deal on a Snap Fitness membership, consider the following tips:

  • Negotiate with the sales team to see if they can offer any discounts
  • Look for promotional periods, such as New Year's sales or summer specials
  • Consider joining during the off-season (usually October to December)
  • Check if you're eligible for the Exercise NZ subsidy scheme
  • Use the 6-week free trial to test the gym before committing to a membership
