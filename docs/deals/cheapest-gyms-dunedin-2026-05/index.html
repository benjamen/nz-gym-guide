Dunedin's Cheapest Gyms

Dunedin has a range of affordable gym options, with prices starting from $10-$20 per week. The Exercise NZ subsidy scheme offers discounts of 40-70% off for eligible members. Here, we compare the most affordable no-contract gym memberships in Dunedin.

Updated May 2026 5 min read

No-Contract Gym Options in Dunedin

For those who prefer flexibility, no-contract gym memberships are available in Dunedin. Anytime Fitness offers a month-to-month membership, while the YMCA provides a sliding scale pricing option. Les Mills also offers a Flexi Membership with no lock-in contract.

The Exercise NZ subsidy scheme is a great way to get the biggest discount on gym memberships. Eligible members can receive up to 70% off their membership fees.

  • Anytime Fitness: month-to-month membership
  • YMCA: sliding scale pricing
  • Les Mills: Flexi Membership
