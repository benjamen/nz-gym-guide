Jetts NZ Review

Jetts NZ is a budget-friendly gym with no joining fee and flexible membership options. With prices starting from $14.95–$19.95/week, it's an attractive choice for those on a budget. But what do you get for your money, and are there any catches?

Updated May 2026 5 min read

What You Get for the Price

For your weekly fee, you get 24/7 access to Jetts NZ gyms, personal training options, and no lock-in contract. This means you can cancel your membership at any time without penalty. The facilities are well-maintained, and the equipment is modern and functional.

However, it's worth noting that Jetts NZ has fewer locations than some of its competitors, which may be a drawback for those who value convenience. Additionally, there are no group classes available, which may be a disappointment for those who enjoy working out in a group setting.

  • 24/7 access to gyms
  • Personal training options
  • No lock-in contract
  • Modern and functional equipment

Contract Terms and Cancellation Policy

One of the major advantages of Jetts NZ is its flexible membership options. There is no joining fee, and you can cancel your membership at any time without penalty. This makes it an attractive choice for those who are unsure about committing to a gym membership or who have irregular schedules.

It's also worth noting that Jetts NZ offers a range of membership options, including a 'no contract' option that allows you to pay week-to-week. This gives you the flexibility to stop and start your membership as needed.

  • No joining fee
  • No lock-in contract
  • Cancel at any time
  • Week-to-week payment options

Current Deals and Promotions

Currently, Jetts NZ is offering a 15% off + 4 weeks free deal for new members. This is a great opportunity to try out the gym and see if it's right for you. Additionally, Exercise NZ's subsidy scheme is available for eligible members, which can provide 40-70% off your membership fees.

It's always a good idea to check the Jetts NZ website for the latest deals and promotions, as these can change over time. You can also compare prices and deals with other gyms in New Zealand to find the best option for your needs and budget.

  • 15% off + 4 weeks free
  • Exercise NZ subsidy scheme (40-70% off)

Pros and Cons

So, who is Jetts NZ best for? It's ideal for those who value flexibility and convenience, as well as those on a budget. The no-lock-in contract and week-to-week payment options make it a great choice for those who are unsure about committing to a gym membership.

However, it may not be the best option for those who enjoy group classes or who prioritize having a wide range of facilities and equipment. Additionally, the limited number of locations may be a drawback for some.

  • Pros: flexible membership options, no joining fee, budget-friendly
  • Cons: limited locations, no group classes
