Gym comparison FAQ

Which NZ gym is cheapest?
City Fitness has NZ's lowest entry price at around $7.99/week (Basic tier), with no lock-in and a one-off $29 joining fee. Jetts and Snap Fitness ($14.95/week) are the cheapest 24/7 options. Prices and joining-fee waivers change often — check current deals.
Which NZ gyms have no contracts?
Jetts, City Fitness, F45, ClassPass, Orangetheory, Contours and 9Round are no-contract gyms — cancel with short notice. Les Mills, Anytime Fitness and Snap Fitness require a minimum term.
Which NZ gym has the best 24/7 access?
Anytime Fitness has the widest 24/7 footprint — 80+ NZ clubs plus thousands worldwide on one membership. Snap Fitness, Jetts and City Fitness also offer 24/7 access at their NZ locations.
How do I compare gym memberships in NZ?
Weigh up weekly price, joining fee, contract length, 24/7 access and features like a pool or classes. Select up to 3 gyms above to see them side-by-side, then read the full review before joining.
