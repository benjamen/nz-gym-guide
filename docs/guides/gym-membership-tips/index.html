10 Tips for Getting the Best Gym Membership Deal in NZ

Gyms are in the business of signing people up on expensive long-term contracts. Here's how to cut through the sales tactics and get a deal that actually suits you.

Updated May 2026 5 min read

Before You Sign Anything

  • Try before you commit — most gyms offer a free trial or day pass. ClassPass lets you try multiple studios for a month free.
  • Visit at your usual workout time — a quiet Monday morning looks very different from a 6pm Tuesday.
  • Ask about the joining fee — it's almost always negotiable, especially at franchise gyms.
  • Read the cancellation clause — know exactly what it takes to exit before you sign.
  • Check if there's a cooling-off period — some NZ gyms offer 7–10 days to cancel without penalty.

Getting the Best Price

  • Sign up in January or June — both are peak promotion periods with waived joining fees and discounts.
  • Sign up online rather than in-club — most gyms offer cheaper rates or no joining fee online.
  • Ask for student, community, or corporate rates — City Fitness and YMCA have the best discount programs.
  • Pay annually — many gyms offer 2 free months on an annual upfront payment.
  • Negotiate the contract length — if a 12-month term is standard, ask if they'll do 6 months first.

Avoiding Hidden Costs

  • Joining fee — can be $49–$149 at premium gyms. Always ask if it can be waived.
  • Access card fee — some clubs charge $20–$30 for the key fob. Ask upfront.
  • Locker rental — check if storage is included or extra.
  • Group class surcharges — at some gyms, premium classes (spin, yoga) cost extra on top of your membership.
  • Personal training upsell — great if you want coaching, but you'll be pressured to buy this at sign-up. It's always optional.
  • Towel hire — easy to forget, usually $2–$4/visit or $5/month extra.

Choosing Between Gyms

The best gym is the one you'll actually go to. Proximity matters more than facilities — a gym 5 minutes from home or work will see far more use than a premium facility 30 minutes away. If you're unsure, ClassPass is the best way to test multiple gyms before committing to one.
