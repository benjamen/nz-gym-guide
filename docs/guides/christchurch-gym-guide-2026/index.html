Christchurch's gym market has seen significant growth since the post-quake rebuild, with City Fitness being a dominant player. However, other options like 24/7 gyms have also gained popularity. Gym Prices in Christchurch

When it comes to gym prices in Christchurch, members can expect to pay between $15 and $40 per week. City Fitness offers a 12-month contract for $24.95 per week, while 24/7 gyms like Jetts and Anytime Fitness offer more flexible contract terms, with prices starting from $15 per week.

Popular Gyms in Christchurch

  • City Fitness (4 locations: Moorhouse Ave, Colombo St, Hornby, and Papanui)
  • Jetts (2 locations: Addington and Riccarton)
  • Anytime Fitness (3 locations: Merivale, Fendalton, and Hornby)
  • Les Mills (1 location: Christchurch Central)
