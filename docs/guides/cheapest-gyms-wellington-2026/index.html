Cheapest Gyms in Wellington 2026 — Full Price Comparison

Wellington has a strong mix of budget chains, full-service clubs, and women-only options across the CBD, Petone, and the Hutt Valley. This guide compares every major Wellington gym by weekly price, contract terms, and 24/7 access so you can find the cheapest membership that actually suits how you train.

By Benjamen Updated June 2026 9 min read

City Fitness — Cheapest Overall in Wellington

City Fitness is the cheapest gym in Wellington at $7.99/week on the Basic plan — under $35/month — with 24/7 access and just a 4-week minimum rather than a long lock-in. The $29 joining fee is regularly waived for online signups. With CBD and Courtenay Place locations, it's well placed for central Wellington workers and students. If price is your only concern, this is the clear winner.

Jetts — Cheapest No-Lock-In 24/7 Option

If you want genuine no-contract flexibility with 24/7 access, Jetts is the best Wellington pick at $14.95/week with no joining fee and online cancellation. Jetts is particularly strong outside the CBD, with clubs in Thorndon, Tawa, Johnsonville, and Lower Hutt — handy if you live in the northern suburbs or the Hutt Valley rather than central Wellington.

Budget Options for Women

Fernwood is Wellington's main women-only gym at around $15/week, offering a supportive, female-only training environment with classes and resistance equipment. For women who prefer a mixed-gym budget option, City Fitness at $7.99/week remains the cheapest, and YMCA Wellington ($13–$20/week) offers a community-focused alternative with pool access at some sites.

Full-Service Options — Les Mills and YMCA

If you want classes, a pool, and a fuller facility rather than the cheapest possible rate, Les Mills Wellington ($17.99–$35/week, CBD and Petone) offers its full timetable of BodyPump, RPM, and BODYCOMBAT classes plus pool access. YMCA Wellington ($13–$20/week depending on membership) is the budget-friendly full-service alternative, with strong community programmes and concession rates.

Cheapest Gyms by Wellington Suburb

Wellington is compact, but the Hutt Valley and Kapiti add real distance — so the cheapest gym you'll actually use depends on where you live.

CBD / Te Aro: City Fitness (Courtenay Place) is the cheapest central option at $7.99/week, with Les Mills Wellington and Snap Fitness nearby for fuller facilities. Best for central workers and Victoria University students.

Thorndon: Jetts Thorndon is the standout no-lock-in 24/7 club for the northern fringe of the CBD and the parliamentary precinct.

Newtown: served by smaller independents and the wider CBD clubs a short bus ride away; budget members often pair a City Fitness CBD membership with the commute.

Lower Hutt: Jetts Lower Hutt leads on value with no lock-in, alongside Anytime Fitness and an F45 studio for class-goers.

Upper Hutt: Anytime Fitness and local independents dominate; 24/7 access matters here given the commute, so Anytime's network is popular.

Petone: Les Mills Petone and Snap Fitness give the waterfront strip both premium and budget 24/7 choices.

Kapiti Coast: Anytime Fitness and independent clubs cover Paraparaumu and Waikanae — chain choice thins out, so 24/7 access and parking usually win over rock-bottom price.

Is the Cheapest Wellington Gym Actually the Best Value?

The lowest weekly price isn't automatically the best deal — value is about cost per visit, not the sticker rate. A $7.99/week City Fitness membership is outstanding value if you train three or four times a week, but if you only manage a couple of sessions a month, a pay-as-you-go option or ClassPass can work out cheaper per workout. Three things separate a genuine bargain from a false economy in Wellington:

  • Location you'll actually use: the cheapest gym across town that you skip on a cold, windy Wellington evening costs more per visit than a slightly dearer club two minutes from home or work.
  • No-lock-in vs annual contract: a marginally cheaper 12-month deal becomes expensive the moment you stop going or leave the city — common for students and contractors. No-lock-in plans protect you from paying through quiet months.
  • What's included: if you'll use classes or a pool, a full-service club like Les Mills or YMCA can beat stacking a budget membership with separate class passes.

For most central Wellingtonians who train regularly, City Fitness genuinely is both the cheapest and the best value. For irregular trainers, the smarter spend is a no-lock-in or casual option.

Student & Corporate Discounts in Wellington

Wellington's large student and public-sector workforce means discounts are widely available — they can cut your effective weekly price by 10–25%.

  • Student rates: Les Mills offers under-23s around 20% off, valuable for Victoria University and Massey students. City Fitness's already-low $7.99/week is effectively a student price in itself. Always ask — many clubs hold an unadvertised student or under-23 rate.
  • Corporate / workplace discounts: with so many government departments and large employers in the CBD, plenty of Wellington gyms run corporate partnerships. Check whether your employer has a deal with Les Mills, City Fitness or Anytime Fitness before paying the standard rate — discounts of 10–20% and waived joining fees are common.
  • Community / concession rates: YMCA Wellington offers concession and community pricing for eligible members, often the cheapest full-service option once a pool is included.

No-Contract Gyms in Wellington

If you're on a visa, studying, or simply unsure how long you'll stay, avoid a 12-month lock-in:

  • Jetts — genuine no-lock-in Flexi membership at $14.95/week with online cancellation; the best no-contract pick across the northern suburbs and Hutt Valley.
  • City Fitness — only a 4-week minimum at $7.99/week, so it behaves like a no-contract gym while being the cheapest in town.
  • Snap Fitness — offers casual/month-to-month options at some clubs alongside its 12-month plan; confirm at your local club.

No-lock-in plans cost a little more per week than a fixed term, but you avoid paying through quiet winter months or after you leave Wellington — almost always the smarter choice for short-term residents.

How to Get the Cheapest Wellington Gym Membership

  • Sign up online — City Fitness, Jetts, and Snap Fitness waive joining fees for online signups
  • Choose by suburb — Jetts covers the northern suburbs and Hutt Valley; City Fitness and Les Mills are CBD-focused
  • Ask about student and under-23 rates — Les Mills gives under-23s 20% off, useful for Victoria University students
  • January and June are the cheapest months to join as gyms run New Year and mid-year promos
  • Avoid lock-in if you're on a visa or unsure — Jetts and City Fitness both let you leave with minimal notice
