Best Gyms in Rotorua 2026 — Prices, Options and What to Expect

Rotorua has a smaller but solid gym market. The main chains — City Fitness, Anytime Fitness, and Snap Fitness — all operate here, giving residents 24/7 access options at competitive prices. The city doesn't have a Les Mills, but the independent gym scene fills some of that gap. For residents and visitors who train regularly, Rotorua has more to offer than most similarly-sized NZ cities.

City Fitness Rotorua

City Fitness Rotorua is the budget anchor of the city's gym market at $7.99/week with no long-term commitment and 24/7 access. If you're looking for the cheapest gym in Rotorua, this is it. The equipment is solid for a budget club — full cardio suite, free weights, and functional training area. The Black Card upgrade ($14.99/week) adds reciprocal access to all City Fitness clubs nationwide — useful if you travel to Hamilton, Auckland, or other cities.

Anytime Fitness Rotorua

Anytime Fitness operates in Rotorua and offers 24/7 access with nationwide reciprocal rights. At $16.95/week on a 12-month contract, it's mid-priced. The advantage over City Fitness is the wider Anytime network — useful for Rotorua residents who travel frequently between cities. The club equipment is reliable and consistent with other Anytime Fitness clubs nationwide.

Snap Fitness Rotorua

Snap Fitness Rotorua sits at a similar price point to Jetts ($14.95/week) but typically requires a short contract period. The 24/7 access and solid equipment make it a good alternative to the bigger chains. Contract terms vary by location — ask about the minimum term before signing.

Independent Gyms in Rotorua

Rotorua has several independent gyms and CrossFit boxes that serve those who want a more community-driven training environment. These typically charge $15–$25/week and offer personal training, group classes, and a different atmosphere from the chain gyms. Worth exploring if you want something beyond a standard equipment-only gym.

Gym Tips for Rotorua Residents

  • City Fitness joining fee ($29) is frequently waived — always check before paying
  • Jetts has no joining fee — $0 upfront to get started
  • Anytime Fitness and Snap both offer 24/7 access — good for shift workers in healthcare and tourism
  • City Fitness Black Card ($14.99/week) is worth it if you travel to other NZ cities regularly
  • Rotorua doesn't have a Les Mills — if group fitness is a priority, ClassPass and independent studios are the alternatives
  • Visiting tourists can usually get a week pass at most Rotorua clubs for around $25–$35

Which Rotorua Gym Is Right for You?

For the cheapest option with 24/7 access and no long-term contract, City Fitness at $7.99/week wins. For people who travel between cities regularly, Anytime Fitness' nationwide network makes the $16.95/week rate worthwhile. Jetts is the best choice if you want zero upfront cost with no lock-in. Rotorua doesn't have Les Mills, so for structured group fitness, look at independent studios or ClassPass.
