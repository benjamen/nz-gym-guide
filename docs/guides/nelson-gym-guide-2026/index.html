Best Gyms in Nelson 2026 — Prices, Reviews & Top Tasman Options

Nelson is the sunniest city in New Zealand and sits at the top of the South Island, covering Nelson city and Richmond (the main commercial suburb). The gym market is healthy for a mid-sized city, with the main 24/7 chains represented alongside strong local gyms and studios. Blenheim (Marlborough) is about 1.5 hours away and not covered here.

By Benjamen Updated June 2026 5 min read

Gyms in Nelson — Overview

Nelson's gym scene covers the essentials well. Snap Fitness and Jetts are both present with 24/7 keyfob access. Anytime Fitness serves those wanting a network gym. The Richmond suburb (15 minutes from central Nelson) has its own gym options as the main commercial zone. Local gyms and CrossFit boxes fill the gap for those wanting coached training. Nelson is also well-served by outdoor fitness options given the climate, but purpose-built indoor gyms are solid.

Snap Fitness Nelson

Snap Fitness has clubs in both Nelson city and Richmond, making it convenient regardless of where you live in the region. Standard Snap setup: 24/7 keyfob access, free weights, cables, cardio equipment. Priced at $19.95–$24.95/week on a 12-month term. Cancellation requires written notice. Good for those who want early morning or after-hours access.

Jetts Nelson

Jetts is the most affordable 24/7 chain gym option in Nelson at around $14.95/week with no lock-in and online cancellation. The lean-but-functional Jetts formula works well for independent trainers who don't need a PT or group fitness classes. For Nelson residents wanting to start a gym habit without a 12-month commitment, Jetts is the logical starting point.

Anytime Fitness Nelson

Anytime Fitness in Nelson is part of the same international network, so membership includes access to any Anytime club globally. Priced at ~$21.99/week on a 12-month term. Equipment is generally well-maintained and covers a broader range than Jetts. The international access benefit is most valuable for Nelson residents who travel regularly to Auckland or abroad.

Nelson YMCA and Community Facilities

Nelson has community fitness facilities including the Nelson City Council aquatic facilities and YMCA-affiliated gyms. These typically operate on staffed hours rather than 24/7 access but offer affordable membership rates ($10–$15/week range) and may include pool access. Good for families, seniors, and those who prefer a community gym environment.

CrossFit and Boutique Studios in Nelson

Nelson has a CrossFit box and several boutique fitness options including F45 and yoga/pilates studios. CrossFit membership in Nelson typically runs $150–$250/month including all coached classes and programming. F45 is priced similarly. Nelson's active, outdoor-oriented population supports a healthy studio scene for its size. Casual drop-in rates at CrossFit are usually $25–$35/session for visiting athletes.

Nelson vs Richmond — Where to Gym?

Richmond is the main commercial hub of the Nelson region and has good gym access including Snap Fitness. Nelson city centre has options but parking can be easier in Richmond. Most chains serve both areas. If you live in Stoke, Tahunanui, or the Maitai Valley, check which club is closest to your home or commute route before committing.
