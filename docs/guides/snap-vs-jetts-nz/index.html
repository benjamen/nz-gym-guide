Snap Fitness vs Jetts — Best Budget 24/7 Gym in NZ 2026

Snap Fitness and Jetts are New Zealand's most accessible budget 24/7 gyms, targeting price-conscious members in smaller towns and cities. Both offer 24/7 access, basic equipment, and minimal frills. Snap is slightly cheaper and often runs joining fee waivers. Jetts has zero joining fee always, plus no-lock-in flexibility. The choice depends on whether you value lowest price (Snap) or maximum flexibility (Jetts).

Price: Same Weekly, Different Joining

Both start at $14.95/week — identical pricing on the surface. The difference is joining fees: Snap charges $49 but frequently runs promotions to waive this fee entirely. Jetts charges nothing — zero joining fee, period. Over the first month, Snap with joining fee costs roughly $109, while Jetts costs $60. If Snap runs a joining fee waiver (common), both cost $60 for the first month. Long-term, if you keep the membership for 12+ months, the joining fee difference becomes negligible — both cost around $777/year.

Flexibility and Contracts

Snap's contract terms are location-dependent — some locations require 12 months, others are month-to-month. Jetts consistently offers no-lock-in contracts everywhere in NZ. If you want guaranteed, national flexibility, Jetts is more reliable. If your local Snap happens to offer no-lock-in, they're equivalent.

24/7 Access — Both Offer It

Both Snap and Jetts are 24/7 with keyfob access. You can train at any hour, on public holidays, Christmas, or 3am. Equipment availability is identical — both are unstaffed facilities with basic cardio, free weights, and resistance machines. Neither has pools, saunas, classes, or staffed support during off-hours.

Equipment and Facilities

Snap and Jetts offer similar basic equipment: cardio machines, free weights, some resistance machines. Quality is consistent across both chains — nothing fancy, but solid and sufficient for solo training. Neither has differentiating facility advantages over the other.

Geographic Coverage

Snap Fitness has slightly better small-town and regional coverage in NZ, with excellent presence across New Zealand. Jetts has good coverage but slightly fewer locations in very rural areas. If you're in a small town and only one 24/7 gym is available, it might be Snap. Both have strong urban and suburban presence.

Who Should Choose Snap Fitness?

  • Price-sensitive members who want maximum joining fee waiver opportunities
  • People in regional towns where Snap is the only 24/7 option available
  • Members wanting an app for tracking workouts
  • Anyone whose local Snap offers month-to-month or no-lock-in terms
  • Those willing to negotiate joining fees (Snap runs promos regularly)

Who Should Choose Jetts?

  • Anyone wanting guaranteed zero joining fee nationwide
  • Members prioritising contract flexibility (no lock-in guaranteed)
  • People wanting simplicity — no uncertainty about contract terms
  • Those wanting to try a gym with absolute minimal risk
  • Members who dislike promotional games and prefer transparent pricing

The Verdict

Snap and Jetts are essentially equivalent products — both $14.95/week, both 24/7, both basic equipment. The decision hinges on joining fees and flexibility. If Snap runs a waiver promotion (frequent), Snap slightly edges ahead on price. If you want guaranteed no joining fee and no-lock-in nationally, Jetts' transparency and guaranteed flexibility make it the safer choice. For most members choosing between these two, pick whichever has the location most convenient to you — the gyms are functionally equivalent.
