Best No-Contract Gym NZ 2026 — Flexible Memberships with No Lock-In

Not everyone wants a 12-month gym contract. If you're new to training, moving cities, working on a visa, or just uncertain about commitment, a no-contract gym membership is the right move. Several NZ chains offer genuine flexibility — here's what the options actually look like.

Jetts — Best No-Contract 24/7 Gym

Jetts is the best no-contract option among 24/7 budget chains in NZ. No lock-in, no joining fee, and a competitive weekly rate of $14.95. You can cancel any time with a short notice period. Jetts has 50+ NZ locations with 24/7 keyfob access. For anyone who wants 24/7 gym access without any financial commitment, Jetts is the top choice — particularly because the $0 joining fee means you're not out of pocket if you decide to stop.

City Fitness — Cheapest No-Contract Option

City Fitness Basic is $7.99/week — the cheapest gym membership in NZ — with just a 4-week minimum (not a lock-in contract). If you can live with a 4-week minimum rather than zero minimum, City Fitness gives you an unbeatable price with genuine flexibility. The Black Card ($14.99/week) adds multi-club access with the same 4-week minimum. Joining fees of $29 are regularly waived, which reduces the upfront cost further.

Contours — Best No-Contract for Women

Contours is a women-only circuit gym with no joining fee and no lock-in. At $9.99/week, it's the second cheapest no-contract option in NZ. The circuit format is beginner-friendly and the sessions are 30 minutes — good for busy schedules. Contours is widely available across NZ cities.

ClassPass — Best for Variety Without Commitment

ClassPass is month-to-month with no joining fee and no contract. From $9/week, you get access to multiple studio classes across participating gyms, yoga studios, pilates studios, and more. If you want variety rather than one gym, ClassPass is the most flexible option in NZ. Credits roll over between months on some plans.

Why No-Contract Gyms Make Sense

  • Moving cities — no exit fee needed, just give notice
  • Working holiday or short-term visa — can't commit to 12 months
  • Testing a new routine — try without being locked in
  • Seasonal training — want to join for summer only
  • Financial uncertainty — can stop if needed without penalties
  • Already tried and failed 12-month gym contracts — try short-term first

The Verdict

For 24/7 no-contract access, Jetts wins — no joining fee, no lock-in, $14.95/week. For the cheapest no-contract option overall, City Fitness Basic at $7.99/week is hard to beat. For women seeking a supportive format with no contract, Contours at $9.99/week is excellent. For multi-studio variety, ClassPass from $9/week is the most flexible option available.
