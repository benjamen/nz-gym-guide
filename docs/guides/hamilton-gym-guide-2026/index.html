Best Gyms in Hamilton 2026 — Prices, Reviews and Honest Guide

Hamilton is one of the most underrated gym cities in New Zealand. Prices are lower than Auckland, the chains are all present, and there are genuine independent options that offer excellent value. City Fitness Hamilton starts at $7.99/week. Les Mills Hamilton is one of the best-equipped Les Mills clubs outside Auckland. And Anytime Fitness has strong multi-club coverage. Whether you want budget 24/7 access or a premium group fitness experience, Hamilton delivers.

City Fitness Hamilton

City Fitness is the standout value option in Hamilton at $7.99/week for a basic membership with no lock-in. The Hamilton club is open 24/7, has solid free weights and cardio equipment, and is genuinely cheap. The Black Card upgrade at $14.99/week adds multi-club access across all City Fitness locations nationwide — useful if you travel. Joining fees ($29) are waived regularly; check current promotions before signing up.

Les Mills Hamilton

Les Mills Hamilton is the premium option in the city. The club at Garden Place is well-equipped with a large group fitness timetable including BodyPump, RPM, Les Mills GRIT, and the full LM suite. At $17.99/week (with a 3-month minimum), it's more expensive than the budget options but the class quality and equipment standard is significantly higher. Les Mills under-23 discount (20% off) is available — always ask. Joining fees of $49 are frequently waived when you sign up in person.

Anytime Fitness Hamilton

Anytime Fitness has multiple Hamilton locations including Central and Te Rapa. The 24/7 access and reciprocal club rights (across all NZ Anytime clubs) make it good value for shift workers or those who travel frequently. At $16.95/week with a 12-month contract, it sits between City Fitness and Les Mills on price. The equipment quality is consistent and reliable. For people who want 24/7 access but want more equipment variety than City Fitness, Anytime is a solid middle ground.

Jetts Hamilton

Jetts Hamilton offers 24/7 access with no lock-in contract and no joining fee — making it the lowest upfront cost option in the city. At $14.95/week, it's close to City Fitness Basic pricing but without any contract commitment. Equipment is functional if not flashy. Good for people who want flexibility without committing to a 12-month deal.

YMCA Hamilton

The YMCA Hamilton is a community-focused gym with family programmes, group classes, and a pool. It's good value for families — the no joining fee structure and 6-month contracts are more flexible than many chains. The facilities aren't as modern as the commercial gyms but the community atmosphere and aquatics access set it apart. Worth considering for families with kids.

F45 and Boutique Options

Hamilton's boutique gym scene is smaller than Auckland or Wellington but growing. F45 Hamilton offers functional training classes at $25+/week. CrossFit boxes and independent studios round out the options for people who want coaching-led training. For studio sampling across multiple formats, ClassPass is available in Hamilton.

How to Get the Best Hamilton Gym Deal

  • City Fitness frequently waives the $29 joining fee — check their website or ask in-person
  • Jetts has zero joining fee — genuinely $0 to start
  • Les Mills joining fees are usually waiveable if you ask when signing up in person
  • Anytime Fitness franchise prices can vary slightly between Hamilton locations — compare both
  • F45 and Orangetheory both offer free trial sessions — try before you commit
  • Student discounts: Les Mills and YMCA both offer reduced rates with student ID
  • Corporate rates: Anytime Fitness has employer deals — check with HR before paying retail

Which Hamilton Gym Should You Choose?

For pure value, City Fitness at $7.99/week is unbeatable — especially with no lock-in and 24/7 access. For group fitness classes and a premium environment, Les Mills Hamilton is the pick. For flexibility without committing to a 12-month contract, Jetts ($0 joining fee, no lock-in) is the easiest entry point. Anytime Fitness suits people who want 24/7 access and travel frequently between cities.
