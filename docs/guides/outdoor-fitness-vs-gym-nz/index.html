Outdoor Fitness vs Gym Membership NZ — Do You Need Both

New Zealand's great outdoors offers a wide range of free or low-cost fitness options, making gym memberships seem like an unnecessary expense for some. With beautiful landscapes and a mild climate, many Kiwis can stay active year-round without stepping foot in a gym. However, there are situations where a gym membership can add significant value to your fitness routine.

By Benjamen Updated June 2026 7 min read

NZ Outdoor Fitness Reality

From running along Auckland's waterfront to cycling through Queenstown's trails, New Zealand offers endless opportunities for outdoor exercise. Many cities also offer outdoor bootcamp sessions, often at a lower cost than gym memberships. For example, a weekly outdoor bootcamp session in Wellington might cost around $10-$15 per session.

When a Gym Membership Adds Value

While outdoor fitness is a great option for many, there are situations where a gym membership can be beneficial. For instance, during the winter months when daylight hours are limited, or for those who prefer to work out in a controlled environment. Gyms like Anytime Fitness, with over 40 locations across NZ, offer 24/7 access and a wide range of equipment, with prices starting from around $17 per week.

Running and Cycling

For those who enjoy running or cycling, the great outdoors is often the best option. However, for those who want to take their training to the next level, a gym membership can provide access to specialized equipment like treadmills and spin bikes. Gyms like World Gym, with locations in Auckland and Christchurch, offer a range of cardio equipment, with prices starting from around $30 per week.

Try Before You Buy

If you're unsure whether a gym membership is right for you, consider trying out a gym before committing to a contract. Many gyms, like F45 Training, offer free trial sessions or guest passes. F45 Training has locations in Auckland, Wellington, and Christchurch, with prices starting from around $50 per week.

Conclusion

While outdoor fitness is a great option for many Kiwis, there are situations where a gym membership can add significant value to your fitness routine. By considering your individual needs and comparing prices, you can make an informed decision about whether a gym membership is right for you.
