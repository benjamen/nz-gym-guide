Snap Fitness vs Jetts NZ — 24/7 Budget Gym Comparison 2026

For Kiwis seeking affordable gym memberships with 24/7 access, Snap Fitness and Jetts NZ are two popular options. With over 80 locations across New Zealand, these franchises offer competitive pricing and flexible contract terms. But which one offers better value for money?

By Benjamen Updated June 2026 9 min read

Overview of Snap Fitness and Jetts NZ

Both Snap Fitness and Jetts NZ operate on a 24/7 keyfob-access model, letting members train at any hour. Both stock cardio machines, free weights, and strength equipment, and both have 50+ clubs across New Zealand. The headline difference is price and contract: Jetts is cheaper at $14.95/week with no lock-in, while Snap Fitness runs $19.95–$24.95/week with slightly better-equipped, more urban clubs. Jetts also has stronger regional coverage, while Snap leans toward larger-city locations.

Pricing Comparison

Jetts is the clear price winner at $14.95/week with no lock-in contract — and it's one of the only NZ budget chains that lets you cancel online. Snap Fitness sits higher at $19.95–$24.95/week depending on the club and whether you take a 12-month or casual plan. Over a year, the difference is roughly $260–$520 in Jetts' favour.

Equipment and Facilities

Both chains cover the basics well — treadmills, bikes, rowers, free weights, cable machines and resistance equipment — and for the vast majority of members either is more than adequate for a solid weights-and-cardio routine.

Snap Fitness clubs are generally slightly larger and better equipped, particularly in main-city locations, with a broader spread of machines, more free-weight stations, and in-house personal training available at many clubs. If you want a wider equipment range or coaching on site, Snap has the edge.

Jetts deliberately keeps clubs lean and functional — a focused free-weights area, the key cardio machines, and the essential resistance kit. That efficiency is part of how Jetts holds its lower price point, and for anyone running a straightforward programme it rarely feels limiting. Neither chain offers pools or saunas, so if those matter you'll want a full-service club like Les Mills or YMCA instead. The practical takeaway: choose Snap if equipment breadth or on-site PT matters; choose Jetts if you want the essentials done well for less.

Locations and Accessibility

  • Snap Fitness: 50+ NZ clubs, weighted toward larger urban centres including Auckland, Hamilton, and Wellington
  • Jetts: 50+ NZ clubs with strong regional coverage across both islands, including Christchurch, Queenstown, and Dunedin
  • Both use 24/7 keyfob entry, so access doesn't depend on staffed hours
  • Jetts is often the only 24/7 budget option in smaller regional towns

24/7 Access — Both Deliver

Both Snap Fitness and Jetts are built around 24/7 keyfob entry, so on round-the-clock access there's no real winner — you can train at 5am or midnight at either. The nuance is the network you can use that fob in. A Snap Fitness membership unlocks Snap clubs across NZ and internationally, which is a genuine plus if you travel overseas. Jetts lets you train at any Jetts in NZ and across the Trans-Tasman network. For most members training in one city, both feel identical; for international travellers, Snap's global footprint edges ahead.

Contract Terms — Both No Lock-In

This is where these two genuinely shine compared with Anytime Fitness or Les Mills: both typically run no-lock-in memberships. Jetts is no-lock-in on its Flexi plan with around 10–14 days notice and — uniquely among NZ budget chains — online cancellation through the app. Snap Fitness is franchise-run, so most clubs offer no-lock-in month-to-month plans (a few still use fixed 3–12 month terms), with a typical 7–14 day notice period and cancellation via the app or your home club. Net result: Jetts is the more predictable no-contract experience nationwide, while Snap's terms depend slightly on the individual franchise — always confirm before signing.

Member App — Jetts Has the Edge

Day-to-day, the app matters more than people expect. The Jetts app is the standout: you can join, freeze, manage and even cancel your membership from your phone, plus track visits — a big reason Jetts feels so low-friction. Snap Fitness also has a member app with workout tracking, club access and account management, and it integrates with Snap's in-house training tools, but cancellation and membership changes more often route back to your home club. If self-service convenience is a priority, Jetts wins the app comparison.

Conclusion and Recommendation

For most people, Jetts wins on the two things that matter most for a budget gym: it's cheaper ($14.95/week vs $19.95–$24.95) and it has no lock-in, with the rare convenience of online cancellation. Choose Snap Fitness if you value slightly better-equipped clubs, more main-city locations, or want in-house personal training. Choose Jetts if price, flexibility, and regional availability are your priorities — it's the better default for shift workers and budget-conscious trainers.
