City Fitness — Cheapest Overall

City Fitness runs a franchise model across Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Hamilton and more. Their Basic membership (from $7.99/week) covers unlimited access to your home club with weights, cardio, and locker rooms. The All-Access membership ($14.99/week) lets you use any City Fitness location nationwide. There's usually no joining fee with online signup — and their minimum term is just 4 weeks, making it one of the most flexible budget gyms in NZ.

YMCA — Cheapest for Families and Beginners

The YMCA charges between $10–$14/week for individual memberships and offers genuine family rates that undercut most chains. If you want a pool, sauna, and crèche at a budget price, the Y is hard to beat. Coverage is limited (mainly Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch and Dunedin) but where they operate, they offer exceptional value.

Jetts — Cheapest No-Lock-In Option

If you want 24/7 access and no long-term contract, Jetts is your best bet in the budget category. They regularly run promotions with no joining fee. The monthly rate works out to roughly $14.95/week — more than City Fitness, but you can cancel anytime with no notice period penalty.

How to Get the Cheapest Gym Membership in NZ

  • Sign up online — most chains offer waived joining fees for online signups.
  • Ask about student, senior, or community rates — City Fitness and YMCA in particular offer discounts.
  • Look for January or June specials — gyms run promotions heavily in both months.
  • Consider a 12-month upfront payment — many gyms offer 2 free months if you pay annually.
  • Try ClassPass first — the first month is free, and you can trial several gyms before committing.
  • Avoid add-ons you won't use — personal training packages are the #1 upsell that blows budgets.
