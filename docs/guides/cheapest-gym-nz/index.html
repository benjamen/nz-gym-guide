City Fitness — Cheapest Overall

City Fitness is the cheapest major gym chain in New Zealand, with Basic memberships at $7.99/week — less than $35/month. The $29 joining fee is regularly waived for online signups. 24/7 access is included at all tiers. The minimum term is just 4 weeks, making it one of the most flexible budget gyms in NZ. Available in Auckland, Wellington, Hamilton, and Tauranga.

Contours — Cheapest Women's Gym

For women, Contours is the best-value gym in NZ at $9.99/week with no joining fee and no lock-in. The 30-minute guided circuit format means no guessing — every session is structured. Available nationwide across all major NZ cities. Free trial week available at most locations.

Jetts — Cheapest No-Lock-In 24/7 Gym

If you want 24/7 access with no joining fee and no lock-in contract, Jetts is the best budget option at $14.95/week. Cancel with just 2 weeks notice. Available in Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Hamilton, and Tauranga.

ClassPass — Cheapest Way to Try Multiple Studios

The ClassPass Starter plan is $39/month (~$9/week) and covers 10 credits — enough for 2–3 yoga, pilates, or HIIT classes at partner studios. The first month is free, making it the cheapest possible way to try different gym formats before committing to one. Not suitable as a full gym replacement unless you go 3+ times per week (the Standard plan at $80/month is better value then).

How to Get the Cheapest Gym Membership in NZ

  • Sign up online — City Fitness, Jetts, and Snap Fitness all offer joining fee waivers for online signups
  • Ask about student, under-23, senior, or community rates — Les Mills gives under-23s 20% off
  • January and June are promo-heavy months — gyms compete hard for New Year and mid-year joiners
  • Consider an annual upfront payment — some gyms give 1–2 months free for annual commitments
  • Use ClassPass free first month to trial several studios before deciding on a permanent gym
  • Corporate rates: check with your employer's HR — Les Mills and Anytime Fitness both offer employer discount schemes
  • Avoid paid PT packages until you've been going 3+ months — they're the #1 upsell that blows gym budgets
