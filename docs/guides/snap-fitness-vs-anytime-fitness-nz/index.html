Snap Fitness vs Anytime Fitness NZ — Which Is Better in 2026?

Snap Fitness and Anytime Fitness are close competitors — both are 24/7 chains at similar price points with similar equipment-focused clubs. The differences are in contract structure, joining fees, and network coverage. If you're deciding between these two, it often comes down to which one is closest to where you live.

Price Comparison

Snap Fitness is slightly cheaper at $14.95/week vs Anytime Fitness at $16.95/week — a saving of $104/year. Joining fees are similar ($49 for both, sometimes waived). The contract terms at Snap Fitness can vary by franchise location — some offer 6-month minimums instead of 12-month like Anytime. Snap Fitness joining fees are frequently waived, making the effective upfront cost lower.

Contract Structure

Anytime Fitness consistently requires a 12-month minimum across NZ. Snap Fitness contract lengths vary by club (some franchise owners offer more flexibility) — it's worth asking your local club whether a shorter minimum is available. If Snap Fitness offers a 6-month option near you, that's a meaningful flexibility advantage over Anytime's 12-month requirement.

24/7 Access

Both chains offer genuine 24/7 keyfob access. There's no meaningful difference here — both let you train at 4am on a public holiday. This is the core offering of both brands.

Club Network

Anytime Fitness has roughly twice the NZ club count of Snap Fitness (100+ vs 50+). Both offer worldwide reciprocal access to their respective global chains. Anytime Fitness' NZ network is larger, making it more likely to have a club near you in smaller cities. Both global networks are comparable in size internationally.

Equipment and Facilities

Both chains run similarly equipped clubs — cardio, free weights, resistance machines, and functional training areas. Neither typically has pools or group fitness studios. The equipment standard at both is consistent across locations. There's minimal practical difference in facilities between a typical Snap Fitness and a typical Anytime Fitness club.

Who Should Choose Snap Fitness?

  • Members where Snap Fitness is closer or more convenient than Anytime Fitness
  • Budget-focused members — $104/year saving over Anytime at base rates
  • Anyone where the local Snap Fitness offers a shorter minimum contract
  • Members who can get the joining fee waived (reducing upfront cost)

Who Should Choose Anytime Fitness?

  • Members in cities or towns where Snap Fitness isn't present
  • Frequent travellers who want the larger Anytime worldwide network
  • Members who prefer the wider NZ club coverage that Anytime provides
  • Anyone who values the Anytime app and fitness tracking tools

The Verdict

Snap Fitness is slightly cheaper and occasionally offers better contract flexibility. Anytime Fitness has a larger NZ network and a better app. In practice, most people choose based on which club is closer to home or work — the difference in price and offering is small enough that location trumps brand preference. If both are equally convenient, Snap Fitness' lower weekly rate and potential for shorter contracts gives it a slight edge.
