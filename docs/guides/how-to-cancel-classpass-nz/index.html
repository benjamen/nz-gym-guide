How to Cancel ClassPass NZ — Step-by-Step Guide 2026

Cancelling your ClassPass membership in New Zealand is straightforward if you follow the right steps. The most important rule: always give written notice and get written confirmation back. This guide gives you the exact process, your legal rights under NZ Consumer Law, and a ready-to-send cancellation email.

Step 1 — Find Your Contract End Date

Before doing anything else, locate your original membership agreement and confirm your contract end date. For ClassPass, the minimum term is Monthly subscription — cancel any time. If you're unsure, call or visit your home club and ask — they are required to tell you. Write the date down before contacting them.

Step 2 — Give Written Notice

ClassPass can be cancelled entirely online through the ClassPass app or website. Go to Account → Manage Membership → Cancel Membership. No phone calls, no in-person visits required. Send your notice with enough lead time to cover the notice period of Cancel at any time before your next billing date. Your notice period starts from the date the gym receives your notice — not when you send it. Always use email so you have a timestamp and a record.

Step 3 — Use This Cancellation Email Template

Copy and paste this template. Fill in the [brackets].

Subject: ClassPass Account Cancellation Request To: support@classpass.com (or use in-app support chat) Hi ClassPass support, I am requesting cancellation of my ClassPass subscription. Account email: [Your email address] Country: New Zealand Requested cancellation date: [Today's date] Please confirm my account has been cancelled and that no further charges will be made after [date]. Thank you, [Your name]

Step 4 — Get Written Confirmation

Do not consider your cancellation complete until ClassPass confirms it in writing. Reply to ask for confirmation if you don't receive it within 3 business days. If the club is unresponsive, escalate in writing to ClassPass's national customer service team. Keep every email.

Step 5 — Cancel Your Direct Debit (At the Right Time)

Only cancel your bank direct debit after you have received written confirmation from ClassPass that your membership has ended. Cancelling before this point is treated as non-payment. ClassPass charges monthly to your card. Cancellation through the app stops future charges immediately. Your membership continues until the end of the current billing period.

Your Rights Under NZ Consumer Law

ClassPass is a US company operating under NZ Consumer Law for NZ subscribers. As a monthly digital subscription, you have the right to cancel at any time. Unused credits are non-refundable under standard terms, but if ClassPass has failed to deliver a service you paid for (studio closed, class cancelled), you may request a refund or credit.

The NZ Commerce Commission has guidance on unfair contract terms — gym contracts that automatically roll over without clear notice, or that charge excessive cancellation fees, may fall foul of the Fair Trading Act 1986. If you believe you're being charged unfairly, contact the Commerce Commission or lodge a dispute with the Disputes Tribunal (free to use for claims under $30,000).

Common Problems and How to Handle Them

  • Subscription auto-renewed before you cancelled — ClassPass sends renewal reminders by email. If you missed it and were charged, contact ClassPass support within 48 hours and request a refund for the unused period.
  • Can't find the cancel button — in the app: tap your profile icon → Settings → Membership → Cancel. On desktop: Account → Membership Plan → Cancel plan. If it's not showing, your account may be on a promotional or gift plan with different terms.
  • Credits disappeared after cancellation — unused ClassPass credits expire at the end of your billing period when you cancel. Use them before cancelling or roll to a lower-credit plan first.
  • Studios showing as unavailable — some studios are removed from ClassPass periodically. This doesn't entitle you to a refund but you can contact support to request a plan adjustment.
