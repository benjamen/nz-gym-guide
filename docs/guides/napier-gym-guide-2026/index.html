Best Gyms in Napier 2026 — Prices, Reviews & Hawke's Bay Options

Napier is the art deco capital of NZ and the main city on the Hawke's Bay coast. The gym market is well-served for a city of its size, with both major 24/7 budget chains and community gyms covering the region. Hastings (20 mins away) shares many of the same gym options, so both cities are covered below.

By Benjamen Updated June 2026 5 min read

Gyms in Napier — Overview

Napier and the wider Hawke's Bay region have solid gym coverage. Snap Fitness and Jetts are both present with 24/7 keyfob access at budget prices. City Fitness has expanded into regional centres and may have a Napier or Hastings club depending on recent openings. The Napier Aquatic Centre provides a public facility option, and several local studios and CrossFit gyms serve the strength and group fitness crowds.

Snap Fitness Napier

Snap Fitness has a well-regarded club in Napier with the full standard 24/7 setup. Free weights, cables, cardio equipment, and a clean training environment. Priced at around $19.95–$24.95/week on a 12-month term. Ideal for members who want early morning or late night access outside staffed hours. Snap also allows home club transfers if you move or travel.

Jetts Napier and Hastings

Jetts operates in the Hawke's Bay area (check their website for the most current club locations as chains open and close). At ~$14.95/week with no lock-in contract and online cancellation, Jetts remains the most affordable 24/7 chain option in most NZ cities. For budget-conscious gym-goers in Napier, this is the starting point.

Anytime Fitness in Hawke's Bay

Anytime Fitness has clubs in the Hawke's Bay region. The main draw is the international network — your membership works at any Anytime Fitness globally, making it useful for frequent travellers. Priced at approximately $21.99/week on a 12-month contract. Equipment and facilities are generally a step above the budget chains.

City Fitness Napier

City Fitness ($7.99–$9.99/week) has been expanding from its Auckland base into regional centres. Confirm current availability via their website. If present in Napier or Hastings, this would be the cheapest option by a significant margin — though their model focuses on value and efficiency rather than premium equipment.

Local Studios and Specialist Gyms

Napier has a solid boutique scene for its size. CrossFit Napier, F45 and various yoga/pilates studios serve those wanting coached group sessions. Casual class rates are typically $20–$35 per session. The region also has Les Mills group fitness integrated into some clubs. For personal training, expect $60–$90/session from independent PTs.

Which Gym Should You Choose in Napier?

For a straightforward choice: Jetts at $14.95/week is the best value 24/7 option for independent training. Snap Fitness is the mid-tier upgrade with a more complete equipment range. Anytime Fitness suits those who travel and want network access. City Fitness — if available — beats all of them on price. For coached sessions, the local CrossFit or F45 studios offer structure and accountability at a premium.
