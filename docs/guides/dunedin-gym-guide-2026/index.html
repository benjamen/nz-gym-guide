Best Gyms in Dunedin 2026 — Prices, Student Discounts and Honest Reviews

Dunedin has a gym market shaped in part by its large student population — prices are competitive and student discounts are common. City Fitness Dunedin is one of the cheapest ways to train in New Zealand at $7.99/week. The University of Otago gym (Recreation Centre) offers excellent student rates. Les Mills and Anytime Fitness serve the non-student market well. This guide covers everything worth knowing about training in Dunedin.

City Fitness Dunedin

City Fitness Dunedin is the cheapest commercial gym in the city at $7.99/week for Basic membership — open 24/7 with no long-term lock-in (just a 4-week minimum). This is particularly popular with non-student adults who want a no-frills 24/7 option without the student-centric environment of the university gym. The Black Card upgrade at $14.99/week adds access to all City Fitness clubs nationwide.

University of Otago Recreation Centre

For Otago University students, the University Recreation Centre (Edgar Centre precinct) is one of the best-value gym options in Dunedin. Semester-based memberships work out to approximately $9/week for full gym and pool access. The student gym environment is energetic and the facilities are well-maintained. Staff memberships are also available for university employees. If you're a student, this is almost certainly your best option.

Les Mills Dunedin

Les Mills Dunedin is the premium commercial option in the city. Located in the CBD, it has the full Les Mills class timetable and solid equipment. At $17.99/week with a 3-month minimum, it's a step up from budget options but the class quality and instructor standard are genuinely better. The 20% under-23 discount makes it considerably more attractive for younger members. Joining fees are often waived when signing up in person.

Anytime Fitness Dunedin

Anytime Fitness has strong Dunedin coverage with clubs in the CBD and suburbs. The 24/7 access and reciprocal club rights across all NZ Anytime clubs make it good for people who travel. At $16.95/week with a 12-month contract, it sits between City Fitness and Les Mills on price. Popular with working professionals who want reliable 24/7 access without the student-heavy atmosphere of the university rec centre.

Jetts and Snap Fitness

Jetts Dunedin offers 24/7 access with no joining fee and no lock-in at $14.95/week — the best flexibility in the market. Snap Fitness also operates in Dunedin at a similar price point with 24/7 access. Both are good alternatives if you want to avoid the 12-month commitment that Anytime Fitness requires.

Student Gym Deals in Dunedin

  • University of Otago Recreation Centre semester rate is around $9/week — beats most commercial options
  • Les Mills offers a 20% under-23 discount — ask when signing up
  • City Fitness sometimes waives the $29 joining fee — always check promotions
  • Jetts has no joining fee at all — best upfront deal for students
  • ClassPass monthly plan ($9–$37/week) lets students try different studios each month
  • Anytime Fitness off-peak memberships are occasionally available for a lower weekly rate

Which Dunedin Gym Is Right for You?

Students: start with the Otago University Recreation Centre — it's the best value by a significant margin. For non-students wanting budget 24/7 access, City Fitness at $7.99/week is unbeatable. For a premium group fitness experience, Les Mills Dunedin is the pick. For maximum flexibility with no joining fee and no contract, Jetts is the easiest entry point.
