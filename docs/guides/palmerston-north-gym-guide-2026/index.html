Best Gyms in Palmerston North 2026 — Prices & Reviews

Palmerston North has a good gym market, boosted by the presence of Massey University and NZDF bases which create a large, active population with real training needs. City Fitness and Les Mills both operate here, giving residents the full range from budget 24/7 to premium group fitness. Student discounts are available at several clubs. If you live in Palmy, you're well-served.

City Fitness Palmerston North

City Fitness Palmerston North is the budget leader at $7.99/week — 24/7 access, no long-term contract, full equipment suite. For anyone who just wants to train affordably without committing to a 12-month deal, this is the clear answer. Joining fees are regularly waived. The Black Card ($14.99/week) adds multi-club access if you travel to Auckland, Wellington, or other cities regularly.

Massey University Recreation Centre

Massey University students have access to the Massey Recreation Centre on campus, which offers gym, pool, and court facilities at student-discounted rates. The semester membership works out to approximately $10/week — very competitive against commercial alternatives. If you're a Massey student, this is almost certainly your best option, especially with the pool and court access included.

Les Mills Palmerston North

Les Mills Palmerston North is the city's premium gym — a full club with the complete LM group fitness timetable. The instructors are good and the equipment standard is strong. At $17.99/week with a 3-month minimum, it's more expensive than City Fitness but the class quality and gym environment are markedly better. The 20% under-23 discount makes it much more competitive for younger members — always ask when signing up.

Anytime Fitness Palmerston North

Anytime Fitness has good coverage in Palmerston North. The 24/7 access and reciprocal rights across all NZ Anytime clubs are the main draw. At $16.95/week on a 12-month contract, it's a middle-ground option — more expensive than City Fitness but cheaper than Les Mills. Popular with NZDF personnel and shift workers who need reliable access at non-standard hours.

Jetts and Snap Fitness

Jetts offers the best flexibility at $14.95/week with no joining fee and no lock-in — useful for Massey students who don't want a 12-month commitment. Snap Fitness operates at a similar price point with 24/7 access. Both are solid alternatives if you want 24/7 access without committing to a year.

Best Palmerston North Gym Deals

  • City Fitness joining fee ($29) is frequently waived — check before you pay
  • Jetts has no joining fee — genuinely zero upfront cost
  • Les Mills under-23 discount: 20% off — ask when signing up
  • Massey Recreation Centre semester rates are competitive with commercial gyms
  • F45 first session free — try before committing
  • Corporate and government rates: Anytime Fitness has employer deals — check with HR or NZDF welfare

Which Palmerston North Gym Is Right for You?

Students: Massey Recreation Centre is the best value, especially with pool access. Non-students wanting the cheapest option: City Fitness at $7.99/week is hard to beat. For group fitness with a full class timetable, Les Mills is the pick. For zero upfront cost and maximum flexibility, Jetts (no joining fee, no lock-in) is the easiest start. For 24/7 access with nationwide club rights, Anytime Fitness is worth the extra cost.
