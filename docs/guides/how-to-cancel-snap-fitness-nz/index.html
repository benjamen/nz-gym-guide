How to Cancel Snap Fitness NZ — Step-by-Step Guide 2026

Snap Fitness is a 24/7 gym and many of its NZ memberships are no-lock-in, month-to-month plans — which makes cancelling fairly painless: a short written notice to your home club and you're done. The catch is that Snap operates as a franchise, so a few clubs still run fixed 3–12 month terms, and the exact notice period (typically 7–14 days, sometimes up to 30) depends on what you signed. This guide covers cancelling via the app or in person, the notice period and direct debit timing, early-exit and medical waivers, freeze alternatives, and your consumer rights — plus a ready email template.

Step 1 — Check Your Contract

Snap Fitness in New Zealand operates as a franchise — each club sets its own contract length, which can range from 3 months to 12 months. Find your original membership agreement or contact your home club to confirm: your start date, your minimum term, and your notice period. The cancellation process described here is standard for most Snap Fitness NZ clubs but always verify with your specific club.

Step 2 — Give Written Notice (App or In Person)

Snap Fitness requires written notice to cancel, and on a no-lock-in membership the notice period is usually short — typically 7–14 days (a few fixed-term clubs require up to 30). There are two reliable methods:

  • Snap Fitness app / member portal: log in, open your membership settings, and submit a cancellation or membership-change request. Screenshot the confirmation.
  • In person: because Snap clubs are 24/7, drop in during staffed hours, complete a cancellation form, and keep a copy. You can also email your home club directly.

Whichever you use, keep a copy of the notice and request email confirmation from the club.

Step 3 — Confirm Final Payment Date

After submitting your cancellation, confirm in writing when your final payment will be deducted. Your membership remains active until the notice period ends — you can continue using the club during this time.

Step 4 — Cancel the Direct Debit

Only cancel your direct debit after receiving written confirmation of your membership cancellation and after your final payment has processed. Cancelling the direct debit early while you still have a balance owing will likely result in debt collection.

Cancelling Early (Before Your Contract Ends)

Snap Fitness clubs handle early cancellations at their discretion as franchise owners. Common grounds for early cancellation without paying out your contract include: - **Medical or injury**: Doctor's letter confirming inability to exercise. - **Relocation to an area without Snap Fitness**: With proof of new address. - **Financial hardship**: At management discretion. If you don't meet any of these criteria, you'll typically be required to pay out your remaining contract period. Contact your club's manager directly and explain your situation before assuming you'll be charged.

Alternatives to Cancelling — Freeze & Transfer

If you only need a break, these options keep your membership (and joining fee) intact:

  • Freeze / suspend: most Snap clubs let you put your membership on hold for travel, injury, or a quiet stretch, usually for a small weekly hold fee. This pauses billing without cancelling and avoids re-paying a joining fee later.
  • Train at another Snap club: your Snap membership gives 24/7 access to Snap Fitness clubs across NZ and internationally — so if you're only travelling, you may not need to cancel at all.
  • Transfer or downgrade: some franchise clubs allow you to transfer the remaining term to another person or move to a cheaper plan. Ask your home club.

What to Do If There's a Problem

If your club isn't processing your cancellation or is charging you incorrectly after a confirmed cancellation, escalate in writing. Contact the Snap Fitness Australia/NZ support team if the club doesn't resolve it. If the issue persists, raise a complaint with Consumer NZ or the Commerce Commission, or lodge a credit card or direct debit dispute with your bank.

Your Rights Under NZ Consumer Law

Because Snap Fitness is a franchise, your contract is with the individual club operator — not Snap Fitness globally. This means:

  • Terms can vary significantly between clubs
  • All fees and minimum periods must have been disclosed at signup
  • If your club closes, you can cancel immediately without penalty
  • The Commerce Commission has previously investigated gym contract practices in NZ — excessive or unclear cancellation fees may be challengeable

Use This Cancellation Email Template

Subject: Membership Cancellation — [Your Name] Hi Snap Fitness [Club], I am cancelling my membership as per the notice period in my agreement. Name: [Name] | Club: [Club] Cancellation date: [As per contract] Please confirm my end date and final charge in writing. Regards, [Name]

Common Mistakes

  • Stopping your direct debit without formal cancellation — this creates a debt balance
  • Not getting written cancellation confirmation from the club
  • Assuming your contract terms are the same as another person's — terms vary by franchise
  • Not checking whether you're still in the minimum term before cancelling
  • Cancelling by text or social media — always use email or in-person written form
