Best Gyms in Wellington 2026 — Honest Prices & Suburb Guide

Wellington's compact geography is a genuine advantage for gym-goers — most people can reach multiple gyms within a 10-minute commute. The market is competitive: City Fitness undercuts everyone on price, Les Mills dominates on group fitness, and the boutique studio scene around the CBD is growing fast. Here's the honest guide to finding your best option.

Wellington CBD & Lambton Quay

The CBD is Wellington's gym heartland. Les Mills on Lambton Quay is the premium choice — popular with office workers doing lunchtime and after-work classes. City Fitness CBD is the budget standout at $7.99/week with 24/7 access — perfect for early commuters or anyone who works non-standard hours near the train station. Orangetheory Wellington is in the CBD and consistently gets strong reviews for weight loss results.

Thorndon & Te Aro

These inner-city suburbs sit between the CBD and the southern suburbs. Several boutique studios operate here — yoga, pilates, and cycling — most accessible via ClassPass. Anytime Fitness has a convenient central location. Snap Fitness covers the 24/7 solo training market at a lower price point than Anytime Fitness.

Newtown & Island Bay

Newtown has a growing fitness scene that reflects its eclectic community. Independent yoga and boxing studios operate alongside chain options. YMCA Newtown is a genuine community facility with pool access. City Fitness has a Newtown presence. For residents in Island Bay and the southern suburbs, these gyms are the most practical — no need to drive into the CBD.

Petone & Lower Hutt

Across the harbour, Petone and Lower Hutt often deliver better value than Wellington city — larger clubs with free parking, at the same or lower prices. Anytime Fitness Lower Hutt is popular with families and shift workers. City Fitness and Snap Fitness both operate in Hutt Valley. For residents who work in the Hutt but want to avoid the CBD commute for gym sessions, this is the practical choice.

Porirua & Upper Hutt

Snap Fitness and Anytime Fitness serve these outer Wellington suburbs well for 24/7 access. Les Mills has a Porirua location. For those on the Kāpiti Coast, options are more limited — Snap Fitness Paraparaumu is the main 24/7 option.

Wellington Gym Tips

  • Wellington's gym market is competitive — always ask about joining fee waivers before paying. Les Mills and Anytime Fitness both run waiver promotions regularly
  • City Fitness Wellington is worth visiting in person — the 24/7 access combined with $7.99/week is hard to beat for budget-conscious Wellingtonians
  • Les Mills Lambton Quay gets extremely busy 12–1pm on weekdays — plan lunchtime sessions for 11:30am or 1:15pm to avoid the rush
  • ClassPass is particularly strong in Wellington — the city has a great selection of yoga, pilates, and cycling studios in the partner network
  • Jetts Wellington has no joining fee — good if you want to try 24/7 training without committing upfront costs
  • YMCA Wellington pool access is available to members — one of the few Wellington CBD pools
