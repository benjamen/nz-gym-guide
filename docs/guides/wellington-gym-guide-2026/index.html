Best Gyms in Wellington 2026 — CBD, Hutt Valley, and Kapiti

Wellington's gym scene is thriving, with over 20 gyms in the city centre alone. With prices ranging from $15 to $50 per week, finding the best value gym can be overwhelming. In this guide, we'll compare top gyms in Wellington, including Les Mills, to help you make an informed decision.

Updated May 2026 7 min read

Wellington Gym Landscape

The Wellington gym landscape is dominated by Les Mills, Anytime Fitness, and Jetts, with multiple locations throughout the city. However, smaller boutique gyms and independent studios are also gaining popularity, offering unique workout experiences and personalized training.

City-Centre Options

  • Anytime Fitness Wellington: $19.95/week, no contract

Hutt Valley and Kapiti Options

For those living in the Hutt Valley or Kapiti region, there are plenty of gym options available. Anytime Fitness has locations in Upper Hutt and Lower Hutt, while Jetts has a location in Paraparaumu. Les Mills also has a location in Lower Hutt.
