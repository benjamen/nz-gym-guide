Home Gym vs Gym Membership in NZ — Full Cost Comparison 2026

With over 500 gyms across New Zealand, including popular franchises like Anytime Fitness and Les Mills, Kiwis have more options than ever to get fit. However, the initial cost of gym equipment can be a significant barrier to setting up a home gym. In this guide, we'll compare the costs of a home gym versus a traditional gym membership in NZ.

Updated May 2026 8 min read

Upfront Equipment Costs

When setting up a home gym, the initial cost of equipment can range from a few hundred to several thousand dollars. For example, a basic weight set can cost around $200-$500, while a treadmill or exercise bike can cost upwards of $1,000-$2,000.

Ongoing Costs

In contrast, traditional gym memberships often come with ongoing weekly or monthly fees. In NZ, these fees can range from $15-$30 per week for a basic membership, with some premium gyms charging upwards of $50-$60 per week. Popular gyms like Jetts Fitness and City Fitness charge around $20-$25 per week for a standard membership.

What You Actually Use

When considering a gym membership, it's essential to think about what equipment and facilities you'll actually use. If you're a casual gym-goer who only uses the treadmill or free weights, a home gym might be a more cost-effective option. However, if you enjoy group fitness classes or require access to specialized equipment like rowing machines or spin bikes, a traditional gym membership might be a better fit.

Motivational Factors

Another crucial aspect to consider is motivation. Some people find that exercising at home can be isolating and demotivating, while others prefer the accountability and social atmosphere of a traditional gym. Popular gyms like Anytime Fitness and Les Mills offer a range of group fitness classes and personalized training programs to help keep you motivated and engaged.
