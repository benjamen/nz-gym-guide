Cheapest Gyms in Christchurch 2026 — Full Price Comparison

Christchurch has a wide spread of budget chains and full-service clubs across the CBD, Riccarton, and the suburbs. This guide compares every major Christchurch gym by weekly price, contract terms, 24/7 access, and student options so you can find the cheapest membership that fits how and where you train.

City Fitness — Cheapest Overall in Christchurch

City Fitness is the cheapest gym in Christchurch at $7.99/week on the Basic plan — under $35/month — with 24/7 access and just a 4-week minimum rather than a long lock-in. The $29 joining fee is regularly waived for online signups. With CBD and Riccarton locations, it's well placed for central workers and University of Canterbury students nearby in Ilam. For pure value, it's the standout.

Jetts — Cheapest No-Lock-In 24/7 Option

For no-contract flexibility with 24/7 access, Jetts is the best Christchurch choice at $14.95/week with no joining fee and online cancellation. Jetts has clubs across multiple Christchurch suburbs, making it convenient whether you're in the central city or the outer suburbs, and the no-lock-in model suits anyone unsure about a long-term commitment.

Student and Concession Options

Christchurch is a major university city, so student value matters. Les Mills gives under-23s 20% off, bringing its weekly rate down meaningfully — useful for University of Canterbury and Ara students. YMCA Christchurch ($12–$18/week) offers community and concession rates and is one of the cheapest full-service options. City Fitness at $7.99/week remains the cheapest flat rate regardless of student status, with no proof of enrolment needed.

Full-Service and Boutique Options

If you want classes and a fuller facility, Les Mills Christchurch ($17.99–$35/week, Riccarton and CBD) offers its full class timetable plus pool access at some sites. YMCA Christchurch is the budget-friendly full-service alternative. At the premium end, Christchurch CrossFit studios run $80–$120/week — far more expensive than the chains, but a different product built around coached, community-based functional training rather than open-gym access.

How to Get the Cheapest Christchurch Gym Membership

  • Sign up online — City Fitness, Jetts, and Snap Fitness waive joining fees for online signups
  • Use student rates — Les Mills under-23 discount (20% off) is significant for UC and Ara students
  • Choose by suburb — Jetts and Anytime Fitness spread across the suburbs; City Fitness and Les Mills cluster in the CBD and Riccarton
  • January and June are the cheapest months to join thanks to New Year and mid-year promotions
  • Avoid lock-in if you're a student or on a short stay — Jetts and City Fitness both let you leave with minimal notice
