Best Women's Gyms in New Zealand 2026 — Honest Guide

Finding the right gym as a woman in New Zealand means weighing up more than just price. Environment, class availability, and whether you want a women-only or mixed space all matter. This guide covers every major option — from dedicated women's gyms to mixed gyms with strong women's communities.

Women's Gym Options in NZ

New Zealand has several gym options specifically marketed to or suited for women. These range from women-only chains (Contours) to premium mixed gyms with excellent group fitness programmes (Les Mills, ClassPass), and community gyms with crèche and family focus (YMCA).

Contours Women's Gym — Best Women-Only Option

Contours is New Zealand's most accessible women-only gym chain, with locations across the country from Auckland to Dunedin. The 30-minute circuit format means every session is guided — perfect for beginners or anyone who doesn't know where to start. Prices from $9.99/week with no lock-in and no joining fee. The welcoming, non-competitive atmosphere makes it the top recommendation for women who've previously felt uncomfortable at mixed gyms.

Les Mills — Best Mixed Gym for Women

Les Mills is consistently ranked the best mixed gym in NZ for women, thanks to its exceptional group fitness programme. BodyPump, BodyBalance, SH'BAM, and yoga classes create a strong community of women at every club. The crèche at most locations is a major drawcard for mothers. Prices from $17.99/week with a 3-month minimum term. Under-23 discounts of 20% apply.

Best Gym for Beginners — Honest Advice

If you're new to the gym and feel nervous or unsure where to start, our honest recommendation is Contours Women's Gym. The guided circuit format removes all uncertainty — you'll never stand in a weights room not knowing what to do. Every session is structured and the environment is specifically designed to be non-intimidating. Once you've built confidence, you can always transition to a mixed gym.
  • Contours — women-only, guided circuit, no pressure, from $9.99/wk
  • YMCA — community feel, pool, crèche, long-standing NZ institution
  • City Fitness Premium — affordable, includes group fitness classes for guided workouts
  • ClassPass — try different formats before committing to a gym type

Women's Gym Safety & Comfort Considerations

  • Women-only gyms have locked, member-access-only entry — Contours and some YMCA women's areas use this system
  • Always check if a mixed gym has women-only changing rooms and toilets (all major chains do, but smaller independent gyms vary)
  • Peak hour crowding at mixed gyms is less common at women's facilities — Contours is rarely busy
  • Ask about trial sessions before committing to any gym — reputable gyms offer these
  • If you're training alone late at night, check lighting and parking at your specific club before signing up
