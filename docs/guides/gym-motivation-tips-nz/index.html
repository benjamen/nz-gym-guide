How to Actually Stick to Your Gym Membership in NZ — Practical Tips

With the average New Zealand gym membership costing around $20-$40 per week, it's essential to make the most of your investment. Many Kiwis struggle to maintain their gym routine, but with the right strategies, you can stay on track and achieve your fitness goals. In this guide, we'll explore practical tips to help you stick to your gym membership in NZ

Updated June 2026 7 min read

Forming a Gym Habit

The key to sticking to your gym membership is to form a consistent habit. This means scheduling your gym sessions at the same time every week and making sure you have a workout routine that you enjoy. Consider starting with small, achievable goals, such as attending the gym twice a week, and gradually increase your frequency as you become more comfortable with your routine.

Accountability Strategies

Having someone to hold you accountable can make a significant difference in your gym attendance. Consider finding a workout buddy or joining a fitness class to help keep you motivated. Many NZ gyms, such as Les Mills and City Fitness, offer group classes and personal training sessions to help you stay on track.
Compare NZ gym prices and classes

Making the Most of Your Gym Membership

To get the most value out of your gym membership, it's essential to understand what's included in your contract. Many NZ gyms offer additional services, such as access to multiple locations, free group classes, and personalized fitness programs. For example, a monthly membership at Jetts Fitness costs around $16.95 per week, while a membership at Anytime Fitness costs around $25 per week.

Tracking Your Progress

Monitoring your progress is crucial to staying motivated and engaged with your gym routine. Consider using a fitness tracker or mobile app to track your workouts, weight, and progress photos. Many NZ gyms also offer fitness assessments and progress tracking as part of their membership packages.
Learn more about fitness tracking at Les Mills

Common Obstacles and Solutions

  • Lack of motivation: Find a workout buddy or join a fitness class
  • Injury or illness: Modify your workout routine or take a break
  • Financial constraints: Consider a month-to-month contract or look for discounts and promotions
  • Lack of time: Schedule your gym sessions in advance and prioritize your workouts

Conclusion

Sticking to your gym membership in NZ requires commitment, accountability, and a clear understanding of your goals and motivations. By forming a consistent habit, making the most of your membership, and tracking your progress, you can achieve your fitness goals and get the most value out of your investment. Remember to compare NZ gym prices and services to find the best option for your needs and budget.
