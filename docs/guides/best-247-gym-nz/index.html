Best 24/7 Gyms in New Zealand 2026 — Always Open, Honest Review

Not everyone can train between 9am and 6pm. Whether you're a shift worker, an early morning person, or just someone who needs flexibility, a 24/7 gym is essential. This guide covers every major 24/7 gym option in New Zealand — price, locations, and who each one suits best.

Updated May 2026 6 min read

NZ Gyms That Are Open 24/7

Four major gym chains operate 24/7 in New Zealand: City Fitness, Jetts, Anytime Fitness, and Snap Fitness. Each uses a key fob or app-based entry system that lets you access the gym any time of day or night, even when staff aren't present.

City Fitness — Cheapest 24/7 Gym in NZ

City Fitness is the best value 24/7 gym in New Zealand at $7.99/week on their Basic plan. Access is 24/7 via key fob. The trade-off is limited locations — Auckland, Wellington, Hamilton, and Tauranga only. If you're in one of these cities and price is your priority, City Fitness is the clear winner.
Read full City Fitness review

Jetts — Best No-Contract 24/7 Gym

Jetts wins on flexibility: no joining fee, no lock-in contract, and 24/7 access from $14.95/week. If you're unsure whether you'll use the gym consistently, Jetts lets you start without any financial risk. Available in Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Hamilton, and Tauranga.
Read full Jetts review

Anytime Fitness — Best for Travellers

Anytime Fitness is the only 24/7 NZ gym chain with global access — your membership works at 600+ clubs worldwide. This makes it unbeatable for frequent travellers. The price is higher ($16.95–$22.95/week) and most locations require a 12-month commitment. The premium is worth it if you travel regularly.
Read full Anytime Fitness review

Snap Fitness — Best in Smaller Towns

Snap Fitness has the best geographic coverage of any 24/7 gym chain in NZ, including locations in smaller towns where City Fitness and Jetts don't operate. Prices start from $14.95/week. Clubs are smaller than Anytime Fitness, but clean and well-equipped for solo training.
Read full Snap Fitness review

Which 24/7 Gym Should You Choose?

  • Best price: City Fitness ($7.99/wk) — but only in Auckland, Wellington, Hamilton, Tauranga
  • Best flexibility: Jetts ($14.95/wk) — no joining fee, no lock-in, cancel anytime
  • Best for travel: Anytime Fitness ($16.95/wk) — 600+ clubs worldwide, works globally
  • Best in smaller towns: Snap Fitness ($14.95/wk) — most geographic coverage in NZ
  • Best no-contract cheapest: City Fitness Basic ($7.99) or Jetts ($14.95) if not in a City Fitness city

Tips for Using a 24/7 Gym

  • Early mornings (5–7am) and late nights (9pm+) are the quietest — great for avoiding peak crowds
  • All 24/7 gyms use key fob or app access — make sure you always have yours before driving to the gym
  • Check your specific club's emergency procedures — even with no staff, emergency call points are required
  • Many 24/7 gyms allow you to trial before you commit — ask about trial periods before signing
  • If you travel interstate or internationally, Anytime Fitness is the only chain with truly global access
