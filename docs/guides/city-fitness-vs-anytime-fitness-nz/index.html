City Fitness vs Anytime Fitness NZ — Which Is Better in 2026?

City Fitness and Anytime Fitness are both 24/7 chains, but they sit at different ends of the budget-vs-network spectrum. City Fitness is cheaper — significantly so. Anytime Fitness has a bigger club network and worldwide reciprocal access. The right choice depends almost entirely on whether you need that network, or whether cheap and local is enough.

Price — The Biggest Difference

City Fitness Basic is $7.99/week — nearly half the price of Anytime Fitness Basic at $16.95/week. Over 12 months that's a saving of $468. Even the City Fitness Black Card ($14.99/week, multi-club access) is cheaper than Anytime Fitness. If price is your primary consideration, City Fitness wins clearly. The joining fees are also in City Fitness' favour ($29 vs $49, both sometimes waived).

Contract Flexibility

City Fitness has no long-term lock-in — just a 4-week minimum. Anytime Fitness requires a 12-month commitment. If your circumstances might change (moving, job change, travel), City Fitness is far lower risk. Leaving an Anytime Fitness contract early typically involves paying out the remainder.

Club Network

Anytime Fitness has approximately 100+ NZ locations vs City Fitness' 30+. Outside major NZ cities, City Fitness coverage is limited — Hamilton, Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Tauranga, Dunedin, and a few others. Anytime Fitness has clubs in many smaller cities and towns. If you live outside a major city, City Fitness may simply not be available near you. Anytime Fitness also includes worldwide reciprocal access to over 5,000 clubs globally — genuinely useful for frequent international travellers.

Facilities and Equipment

Both chains are equipment-focused with no pools or group fitness studios. The equipment standard at City Fitness is solid for the price — you get cardio, free weights, and functional training at a standard that matches most Anytime Fitness clubs. Anytime clubs tend to be slightly larger with more machine variety, but the practical difference for most members is minimal. Neither chain will impress with premium facilities.

Who Should Choose City Fitness?

  • Budget-conscious members — $7.99/week is hard to beat anywhere
  • Members in major NZ cities where City Fitness has coverage
  • Anyone who wants 24/7 access with no long-term commitment
  • Solo trainers who don't need group fitness or premium facilities
  • People who don't travel internationally and don't need worldwide club access

Who Should Choose Anytime Fitness?

  • Members in smaller cities and towns where City Fitness isn't present
  • Frequent international travellers who want worldwide club access
  • Members who want the widest possible NZ club coverage
  • People who prefer the slightly larger clubs that Anytime typically offers
  • Anyone travelling between multiple NZ cities regularly

The Verdict

City Fitness is the better deal for the vast majority of NZ members. The $7.99/week Basic rate with no lock-in and 24/7 access is exceptional value — you save $468/year vs Anytime Fitness and have more flexibility. The only reasons to choose Anytime Fitness are: you live in a city or town where City Fitness doesn't have a club, or you travel internationally regularly and want worldwide club access. If neither of those applies, City Fitness is the smarter choice.
