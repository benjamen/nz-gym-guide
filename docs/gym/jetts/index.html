Jetts NZ — Full Membership Review 2026

Jetts Fitness arrived in New Zealand as part of an Australian franchise expansion and has established itself as the go-to option for NZ gym-goers who prioritise flexibility over facilities. The no joining fee, no lock-in proposition is genuinely differentiated in a market where Anytime Fitness requires 12-month commitments and Les Mills demands 3-month minimums. For people who've been burned by unused memberships before, Jetts is the psychologically easy choice.

What You Actually Get for $14.95–$19.95/Week

A Jetts membership gets you 24/7 keycard access to the gym floor: treadmills, ellipticals, bikes, cable machines, free weights, and fixed weight machines. The club size varies significantly — city locations like Jetts Auckland CBD are spacious; some suburban franchises feel small and under-equipped. Equipment quality is generally good — Life Fitness and Technogym machines appear at most locations — but the range is narrower than a full-service gym. There is no group fitness, no pool, no sauna, and at most clubs no on-site staff during unmanned hours.

The key practical question is whether your specific local Jetts is worth the money. Equipment maintenance varies by franchise operator — some clubs are very well-maintained, others have multiple broken machines. Reading Google reviews for your specific location (not the brand overall) is essential before signing up.

Jetts vs Anytime Fitness — The Decision Most NZ Gym-Goers Face

These two gyms compete directly for the same customer: people who want 24/7 access, no group classes, and a no-frills training environment. The differences that actually matter:

  • Entry cost: Jetts has no joining fee; Anytime Fitness charges $49 upfront. If you're unsure you'll stick with it, Jetts costs $49 less to try.
  • Lock-in: Jetts has none on the Flexi plan; Anytime Fitness requires 12 months. This is a significant difference — you can't exit an Anytime Fitness membership without paying out the remaining term or negotiating.
  • Coverage: Anytime Fitness has approximately 80 NZ locations vs Jetts' ~30. If you travel between cities or move suburbs, Anytime Fitness is more useful.
  • Global access: Anytime Fitness works at 6,000+ clubs worldwide. Jetts operates primarily in Australia and NZ. For international travellers, Anytime Fitness wins easily.
  • Price: Both start at $14.95/week for single-club access. The difference appears at the premium tier — Jetts $19.95 vs Anytime $22.95 for multi-club access.

Bottom line: Jetts wins on flexibility and low entry cost. Anytime Fitness wins on coverage and international access. If you're confident you'll use a gym regularly for 12+ months, Anytime Fitness is fine. If you're uncertain or value the ability to walk away easily, Jetts is the rational choice.

Jetts Franchise Quality — What Members Say

Jetts operates as a franchise, which means quality varies significantly between clubs. The brand sets minimum equipment standards, but the day-to-day experience — cleanliness, equipment maintenance, member culture — depends heavily on the individual franchise operator.

Auckland CBD and Newmarket Jetts locations consistently score higher in member reviews than some suburban locations. Hamilton and Tauranga Jetts clubs are generally well-rated. The worst experiences come from older suburban clubs where the franchise operator hasn't reinvested in equipment refresh. Before signing up, visit your intended club on a Monday evening (busy time) to check cleanliness and working equipment.

Who Should Join Jetts NZ?

Jetts is ideal for: First-time gym members who aren't sure they'll stick with it (no risk if you quit), people who train solo without wanting PT or classes, anyone who's had bad experiences with lock-in contracts, budget-focused trainers who don't need facilities beyond weights and cardio.

Jetts is not ideal for: Group fitness enthusiasts (there are none), families who want childcare or a pool, members who travel frequently between smaller NZ cities where Jetts doesn't have a club, anyone who wants instructor-led classes or regular PT.

How to Get the Best Jetts Membership Deal

Jetts runs periodic promotional offers — joining fee waivers are their main promotional lever (though they normally have no joining fee anyway). The most reliable way to get the best deal is to visit in person and ask if any current promotions are running. Corporate and student discounts exist at some locations — ask specifically. The Flexi plan at $14.95 is the entry point; the Premium plan at $19.95 adds reciprocal access to all Jetts locations, which is worth it if you regularly use multiple locations or travel between cities where Jetts has clubs.

Jetts NZ Cancellation — Your Rights

Jetts cancellation requires 2 weeks written notice. Email your home club directly with a written cancellation request. You will be charged for the 2-week notice period. There is no lock-in contract on the Flexi plan — you can cancel from day one without penalty beyond the 2-week notice fee. Keep a copy of your cancellation email. If you're on the Flexi plan, this is one of the simplest gym cancellations in NZ — no minimum term, no exit fees, no negotiation required.

Jetts NZ — Frequently Asked Questions

Does Jetts NZ have a joining fee?

No — Jetts has no joining fee. You pay your first week upfront and then weekly after that. This makes it one of the cheapest gyms to start at in NZ with no upfront barrier.

Can I cancel Jetts anytime?

Yes. Jetts requires 2 weeks written notice to cancel. Unlike many competitors, there is no minimum lock-in period on their Flexi membership. Simply notify your home club in writing (email is accepted).

How many Jetts locations are there in NZ?

Jetts has approximately 30 locations in New Zealand, concentrated in Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Hamilton and Tauranga. Coverage is thinner in smaller cities compared to Anytime Fitness (~80 clubs).

Does Jetts have group fitness classes?

No — Jetts does not offer group fitness classes at most NZ locations. It is a self-service 24/7 gym focused on weights and cardio equipment. If group classes are important to you, consider Les Mills or F45.

How does Jetts compare to Anytime Fitness NZ?

Both are 24/7 no-frills gyms but Jetts has no joining fee and no lock-in (Anytime requires 12 months). Anytime Fitness has roughly 2.5x more NZ locations and some clubs include group classes. For pure flexibility and lowest entry cost, Jetts wins. For nationwide coverage, Anytime Fitness is better.

Can I use all Jetts locations with one membership?

On the Flexi plan you access only your home club. The Premium plan ($19.95/week) gives reciprocal access to all Jetts NZ locations. If you travel often between cities, the Premium plan is worth the extra $5/week.

Is Jetts good for beginners?

Yes — the no joining fee and no lock-in make Jetts a low-risk option for first-time gym-goers. The downside is no group classes or instructors to guide you. Many beginners start at Jetts then move to a gym with PT or group fitness once they know what they want.

Does Jetts have personal training?

Yes. Jetts clubs offer personal training sessions with on-site trainers, typically charged per session or in blocks. PT is not included in the membership fee. Expect to pay $60–$90 per 45-minute PT session.
