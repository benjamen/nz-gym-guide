Is P3 Recovery NZ Worth It? Quick Answer

Auckland's favourite recovery destination. Float tanks, infrared sauna and ice baths make P3 the complete recovery hub for athletes and professionals alike. Best for: Float therapy, infrared sauna, ice bath recovery, stress relief. Watch out for: Book float sessions well in advance — very popular.
