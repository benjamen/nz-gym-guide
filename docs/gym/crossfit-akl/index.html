Is CrossFit AKL Worth It? Quick Answer

The go-to CrossFit affiliate in central Auckland. Strong programming, qualified coaches and a supportive community that caters to all fitness levels. Best for: CrossFit, functional fitness, competitive athletes and beginners alike. Watch out for: Can be intense for complete beginners — foundations course recommended.

Cost: $40.0–$55.0/week. Joining fee: $0 (No joining fee).
