Is ClassPass Worth It? Quick Answer

Best for variety — access yoga, pilates, HIIT, cycling and more on one plan. Best for: Variety seekers, travelers, trying different fitness styles. Watch out for: Credits expire monthly. Popular classes book out fast..

Cost: $9.0–$37.5/week. Joining fee: $0 (First month free trial).
