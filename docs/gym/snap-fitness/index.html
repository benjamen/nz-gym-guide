Is Snap Fitness Worth It? Quick Answer

Solid 24/7 option especially in smaller NZ towns where other chains don't reach. Best for: Simple 24/7 gym access, small-town NZ locations. Watch out for: Very small clubs — can feel cramped during peak hours..
