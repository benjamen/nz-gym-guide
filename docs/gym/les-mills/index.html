Les Mills NZ — Full Membership Review 2026

Les Mills is the dominant premium gym chain in New Zealand. Founded in Auckland in 1968 by Olympic weightlifter Les Mills, it grew into a national fitness institution before launching its group fitness programmes globally. Today, Les Mills group fitness formats (BodyPump, BodyCombat, RPM) are licensed to gyms in 100+ countries — but the NZ clubs where those formats were developed remain, by most accounts, the best gym membership in the country for people who want group fitness, pools, and quality facilities.

What You Get for $17.99–$24.99/Week

A standard single-club membership gets you: full access to the gym floor with weights, cardio and functional training equipment; the complete group fitness timetable (80–120 classes per week at major clubs); pool and sauna access at most full-service locations; crèche access during specified hours; and the Les Mills companion app for booking classes. At premium clubs (Auckland City, Takapuna, North Harbour, Wellington City), the facilities are genuinely world-class — 25m pools, modern equipment, spacious studios, high-quality changing rooms.

The all-clubs membership ($24.99/week) adds access to all Les Mills NZ locations. This is useful if you train near the office during the week and near home on weekends, or travel between major cities.

The Problem With Les Mills NZ: Pricing Transparency

The single biggest frustration cited by NZ gym shoppers is that Les Mills does not publish membership prices on their website. You must visit a club or call to get pricing. This is a deliberate strategy — it allows each club to quote based on demand, competitor pricing, and promotional context. The prices in this review are based on confirmed quotes and member reports, but Les Mills can and does vary pricing by location and time of year.

The practical implication: always ask about the joining fee waiver (it's almost always available if you ask — $49 is not commonly collected at sign-up), ask about the under-23 discount if relevant, and ask about any current promotions. The person at the desk has latitude to offer deals, especially at quieter times of year.

Les Mills Group Fitness — Why It's the Best in NZ

Les Mills group fitness classes are genuinely the best available in any NZ gym chain. This isn't brand loyalty — it's the result of professionally produced, globally tested formats with consistent instructor certification standards. A BodyPump instructor at Les Mills Auckland has trained to the same standard as BodyPump instructors in New York and London. The format design is sophisticated: music licensing, choreography, and progressive muscle loading are all professionally managed at a global level that no independent studio replicates.

For people who want group fitness as their primary training modality, Les Mills has more variety (100+ class formats), more sessions per week, and better instruction quality than any competitor in NZ. This is the core reason to pay the premium over City Fitness or Jetts.

Les Mills NZ vs Alternatives — When the Premium Is Worth It

Les Mills beats competitors when: You want group fitness classes as a core part of your routine, you use the pool or sauna regularly, you need crèche access, you want the flexibility to train at multiple clubs, or you're under 23 (the student/youth discount makes it competitive with mid-range gyms).

Alternatives win when: You primarily train alone with weights (City Fitness or Jetts are $5–10/week cheaper for the same floor access), you can't commit to a 3-month minimum (Jetts and City Fitness have no lock-in), you only need basic facilities (City Fitness Basic at $7.99 is far cheaper for similar equipment access), or you travel frequently to cities without Les Mills clubs (Anytime Fitness is better networked in smaller NZ cities).

Les Mills NZ Contract Terms — What to Know Before Signing

The 3-month minimum term is Les Mills' most-discussed feature. After the minimum, you continue on a rolling basis — technically month-to-month with 4-week notice to cancel, charged weekly. The key practical points:

  • You cannot cancel during the 3-month minimum without paying out the remaining term, unless you have medical grounds (doctor's certificate required)
  • Membership freezes are available for medical, travel, or hardship reasons — typically 4 weeks minimum, up to 3 months per year, at a reduced holding fee of $5–10/week
  • Transfers between Les Mills clubs are generally accommodated when moving cities
  • Corporate/employer rates are available at many companies — check with your HR team, as this can reduce the price by 10–20%

Les Mills NZ — Which Clubs Are the Best?

Quality is consistent across the chain but the flagship clubs stand out: Les Mills Auckland City (99 Graham Street) is the best-equipped gym in Auckland, with the largest pool and most complete facilities. Les Mills North Harbour (Albany, Auckland) is purpose-built with excellent parking. Les Mills Wellington City is central and well-maintained. Smaller city clubs in Hamilton and Tauranga are good but lack the pool facilities of the flagship clubs. Always visit your specific local club before signing — some suburban locations feel noticeably smaller than the flagship clubs shown in marketing materials.

How to Get the Best Les Mills Membership Deal in NZ

Les Mills' main promotional levers are: joining fee waivers (almost always available — always ask), the under-23 discount (20% off), student discounts (ID required), and periodic new member promotions (typically January and mid-year). Corporate rates via employer are often 10–20% off standard pricing. The $49 joining fee is rarely actually charged to members who ask — mention a competitor quote when signing up and the joining fee will usually be waived. Don't pay it without asking.

What Members Say — Les Mills Review Summary

Les Mills NZ reviews are strongly positive for the quality of group fitness classes — BodyPump and BodyCombat consistently get 5-star mentions. The biggest complaint is the sign-up process: pricing not being published online and being required to visit in person is a common frustration cited by 3-star reviewers. Pool and sauna access is praised highly. Some members at suburban clubs mention equipment maintenance issues. The crèche is a key differentiator for parents and drives several 5-star reviews specifically.

Les Mills NZ — Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Les Mills cost per month in NZ?

A single-club Les Mills membership costs $17.99/week, which equals approximately $77.96/month. The all-clubs membership is $24.99/week ($108.29/month). The joining fee of $49 is often waived during promotional periods — ask at sign-up.

Do you have to sign a contract at Les Mills NZ?

Yes — Les Mills requires a minimum 3-month commitment. You cannot sign up month-to-month from day one. After the 3-month minimum, you continue on a rolling weekly basis and can cancel with 30 days written notice. This is Les Mills' biggest drawback compared to no-lock-in gyms like Jetts or City Fitness.

Can I try Les Mills before joining?

Yes — Les Mills NZ frequently offers free trial passes (1–7 days) via their website or when you visit in person. Promotions change monthly. The safest way is to ask at reception for a free day pass rather than committing to a membership on first visit.

Is the Les Mills app included in the membership?

The Les Mills+ streaming app (for home workouts) requires a separate subscription ($19.99/month or $159/year). It is not included with a club membership. However, you can access on-demand versions of class tracks as a club member through the companion app.

Does Les Mills have a pool in NZ?

Most Les Mills clubs in Auckland, Wellington and Christchurch have 25-metre heated pools. Not all suburban or smaller city clubs have pools — check with your specific club before joining if the pool is important to you.

What discount does Les Mills offer students and under-23s?

Les Mills offers 20% off for members under 23 years old. Student discounts are also available with valid student ID. These discounts bring the single-club membership to approximately $14.39/week — competitive with Jetts.

Can I freeze my Les Mills membership?

Yes — Les Mills allows membership freezes for medical reasons, travel, or other circumstances, usually for a minimum of 4 weeks and a maximum of 3 months per year. A small admin fee ($5–$10/week) may apply. Contact your home club directly to arrange a freeze.

How do I cancel my Les Mills membership in NZ?

You must provide 30 days written notice after the minimum 3-month term has passed. You cannot cancel by phone — written notice (email or letter to your home club) is required. Keep a copy of your cancellation notice. For a full cancellation guide, see our Les Mills cancellation guide.
