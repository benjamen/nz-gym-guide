Is CrossFit Central Wellington Worth It? Quick Answer

Wellington's highest-rated CrossFit box with a 5.0 Google rating from 53 reviews. The 40+ sessions per week with no booking system gives unbeatable flexibility. Serious about functional fitness. Best for: Serious CrossFit athletes and those wanting maximum flexibility with no booking. Watch out for: CrossFit is high intensity — not suitable for complete beginners without foundations first.

Cost: $52.0–$62.0/week. Joining fee: $0 (Couples rate $58/week each. Student rate $52/week.).
