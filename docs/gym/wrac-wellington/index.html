Is Wellington Regional Aquatic Centre (WRAC) Worth It? Quick Answer

Wellington's flagship aquatic facility in Kilbirnie. A 50-metre heated pool, diving pool, hydrotherapy pool, spa, sauna, and ClubActive gym under one roof. At $22.10/week with no joining fee, the best-value facility for swimmers and families in Wellington. Best for: Lap swimmers, families, aqua fitness participants, and hydrotherapy users. Watch out for: Public facility — can get busy during school holidays and peak times. Book lanes ahead..

Cost: $22.1–$22.1/week. Joining fee: $0 (No joining fee. LeisureCard holders get 20% off. Casual swim also available.).
