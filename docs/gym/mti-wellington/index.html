Is MTI Wellington — Muay Thai Institute Worth It? Quick Answer

Wellington's most decorated combat sports gym, producing 35+ national and international champions. 28 classes/week from beginner Thai boxing to championship fight training. The $40–44/week membership also includes 24/7 weights gym access — exceptional value. Best for: Anyone wanting to learn Muay Thai, MMA or boxing — beginner to competitive fighter. Watch out for: Combat sports training — contact sport. Not suitable if you want zero contact..

Cost: $30.0–$44.0/week. Joining fee: $0 (Under 18: $30/week. Adults: $40–$44/week. Includes 24/7 gym access.).
