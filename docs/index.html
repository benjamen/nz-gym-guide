Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest gym in NZ?

City Fitness is the cheapest major gym chain in NZ, starting from $7.99/week on their Basic membership. Jetts offers no-lock-in memberships from around $14.95/week. See our cheapest gym guide for a full breakdown.

Which NZ gyms have no contracts?

City Fitness (4-week minimum), Jetts (cancel anytime), F45 (most studios), and ClassPass (monthly subscription) all offer flexible memberships with no long-term lock-in. Les Mills and Anytime Fitness typically require 3–12 month terms.

Is ClassPass worth it in New Zealand?

ClassPass is excellent if you like variety and don't want to commit to one gym. You get access to hundreds of NZ studios for yoga, HIIT, pilates, cycling and more. The Starter plan from $36/month suits occasional gym-goers; the Premium plan suits those doing 3+ sessions per week. Read our full ClassPass NZ review.

Can I cancel a gym membership early in NZ?

Under the Consumer Guarantees Act, you can cancel if the gym moves location, reduces services, or you have a medical reason. For other reasons, most gyms require you to pay out the remaining months or find a member to take over your contract. See our how to cancel guide.

What gym is best for beginners in NZ?

ClassPass is great for beginners — try different styles before committing. For a permanent gym, City Fitness offers an affordable, low-pressure environment. F45 or Les Mills group classes are excellent if you want structure and coaching.
