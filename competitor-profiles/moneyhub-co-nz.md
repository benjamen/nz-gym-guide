## Competitive Implications for NZ Gym Guide

**Where they're strong vs. us**: Domain authority — their gym pages rank partly because of spillover from financial content. We can't match their DA.

**Where we're strong vs. them**: Depth. We have per-gym FAQs, pros/cons, structured schema, a quiz, diet plans, and a specialist identity. We're the gym expert; they're the money expert who also covers gyms.

**Opportunities**:
- Target long-tail gym queries MoneyHub doesn't cover (e.g., "best gym for weight loss NZ", diet plan + gym combos)
- Differentiate on interactivity (quiz) and depth (FAQPage schema → rich results)
- Earn featured snippet positions MoneyHub's shallow content can't hold

**Threats**:
- If MoneyHub deepens their gym content, their DA advantage becomes crushing
- They could add a quiz or comparison table with minimal effort
