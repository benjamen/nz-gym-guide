## Key Takeaways

1. **No specialist competitors** — MoneyHub and Canstar are financial giants covering gyms tangentially. A dedicated gym guide with depth can win long-tail gym queries they can't hold.

2. **Schema is our weapon** — Neither competitor uses FAQPage or per-gym structured data. Our FAQ schema creates rich result eligibility they can't match without significant development.

3. **Quiz is a moat** — "Find My Gym" quiz has no equivalent at either competitor. This is a high-intent, session-extending feature that builds engagement and internal link equity.

4. **Diet content cross-sell** — Unique to us. No competitor links gym advice to diet plans. This creates a content cluster that extends session depth and topical authority.

5. **Authority gap is real** — MoneyHub and Canstar will outrank us for head terms ("gym NZ", "cheapest gym NZ") until our DA grows. Focus on long-tail and structured-data-eligible queries in the short term.

## Gaps and Opportunities

- **Supplement/protein content** — Neither competitor covers gym nutrition. Cross-linking diet plans to supplement guides could capture "best protein powder NZ" queries.
- **City-specific deep dives** — Both competitors' city pages are thin. We can win "Auckland 24/7 gym" or "Wellington no-contract gym" with depth.
- **Video content** — Neither competitor has YouTube. Gym tour videos + YouTube schema could capture Google AI Overview citations.
- **Cancel gym membership** guides — High-intent, competitor-free territory.
- **ClassPass alternative / review** — Niche but no NZ specialist covers it.
