**Generated**: 2026-05-29

## Situation

The site has strong foundational coverage: 11 gym chain pages, 9 city hub pages, gym×city intersection pages, a quiz, and 14 guide posts. The main gaps are:

- 6 city guides missing (Hamilton, Tauranga, Dunedin, Rotorua, New Plymouth, Palmerston North)
- No per-chain "how to cancel" content — high-intent, low-competition
- No goal-specific gym recommendation content (seniors, under-18s, weight loss focus, no-contract)
- Only 3 head-to-head comparisons written; 7 more gym pairs have real search volume
- No content targeting the "is it worth it" angle for mid-tier chains

---

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: City Gym Guides
**Why**: "gyms in [city] NZ" is the highest-volume, most commercial intent query in the space. Auckland, Wellington, and Christchurch are covered; six cities are not.

### Pillar 2: Gym Comparisons ("X vs Y")
**Why**: Comparison queries convert at 5–15%. Competitor profiles showed MoneyHub and Canstar cover these shallowly. Real data + honest verdicts win here.

### Pillar 3: Goal-Specific Gym Selection
**Why**: Searchers with a specific goal (lose weight, no contract, seniors) have clearer buying intent than generic searchers. These pages also rank for long-tail clusters.

### Pillar 4: Membership & Contract Guides
**Why**: Gym contracts are a major objection. "How to cancel X gym NZ" queries are high-volume, near-zero competition, and attract churning members who may re-join via the site.

### Pillar 5: Fitness Goals + NZ Context
**Why**: The diet section already exists. Extending into fitness goal content (home gym vs commercial gym, free gym alternatives) builds topical authority around health + fitness.

## Content Format Standards

All guides follow the existing site structure:
- Quick-answer box at top (40–60 word direct answer)
- Comparison table (structured data for AI extraction)
- Honest pros/cons per option
- FAQPage schema (4 questions minimum)
- BreadcrumbList schema
- Internal links: chain page → city page → guide → comparison
- CTA: quiz or compare page
