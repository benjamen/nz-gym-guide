Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest gym in NZ?

City Fitness is the cheapest major gym chain in NZ, starting from $7.99/week on their Basic membership (24/7, no lock-in, $29 joining fee — often waived online). Contours Women's Gym is the cheapest for women at $9.99/week with no joining fee or lock-in. See our cheapest gym guide for a full breakdown.

Which NZ gyms have no lock-in contracts?

City Fitness (4-week minimum), Jetts (2-week notice, no lock-in), Contours (2-week notice), 9Round (monthly), F45 (most studios), and ClassPass (monthly subscription) all offer memberships with no long-term lock-in. Les Mills and Anytime Fitness typically require 3–12 month commitments.

What's the best gym for weight loss in NZ?

Orangetheory Fitness and F45 Training consistently deliver the strongest weight loss results, due to coached HIIT sessions and built-in accountability. Orangetheory's heart rate monitoring system ensures every session targets the fat-burning zone. Both offer a free first class — try before you commit.

Is ClassPass worth it for yoga and pilates in NZ?

Yes — ClassPass is the most cost-effective way to access yoga, pilates, barre, and other studio classes in NZ. The Standard plan (~$80/month) covers 6–8 studio classes, which would cost $140–$200+ at casual rates. Partner studios include most major yoga and reformer pilates studios in Auckland, Wellington, and Christchurch. Read our full yoga & pilates guide.

What gym is best for beginners in NZ?

For women: Contours Women's Gym — guided 30-min circuits, women-only, no intimidation, from $9.99/week. For mixed gyms: City Fitness is affordable and low-pressure, or use ClassPass to trial different styles before committing to one format. Take our quiz for a personalised recommendation.

Can I cancel a gym membership early in NZ?

Under the Consumer Guarantees Act, you can cancel if the gym moves location, reduces services materially, or you have a medical reason. Otherwise most gyms require you to pay out remaining months or find a member to take over your contract. See our how to cancel guide for city-specific advice.

Which NZ gym is best for 24/7 access?

City Fitness ($7.99/wk), Jetts ($14.95/wk, no lock-in), Snap Fitness ($14.95/wk), and Anytime Fitness ($16.95/wk) are all 24/7. Anytime Fitness is the only chain with global 24/7 access — useful if you travel overseas. See our 24/7 gym guide for a full comparison.
